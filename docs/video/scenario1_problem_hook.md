# Scenario 1 — Problem Hook (Real-Life Footage)

**Setting:** Klinik Kesihatan in **Long Pa Sia, Sipitang District, interior Sabah** — a small highland village near the Sarawak–Brunei border, reachable only by a 2–3 hour drive on unpaved logging roads. No mobile signal in most of the area. The nearest hospital (Sipitang/Lawas) is over an hour away by car — and most villagers don't own one. This clinic is staffed by a single doctor and serves several surrounding longhouses scattered across the highlands.

**Characters:** Dr. Satish Rao (the lone doctor on duty), Zhu Heng (patient, villager from a longhouse ~10km away)

---

## Shot 1 — The Clinic, Early Morning

Dr. Satish Rao is alone at his desk in a small wooden-walled clinic room — a fan creaking overhead, a stack of CPG booklets beside his laptop. He's reviewing notes from the previous day. Quiet. No nurses, no other staff in frame.

---

## Shot 2 — Zhu Heng Arrives

The door opens. Zhu Heng — a villager in his late 50s — steps in slowly, breathing hard, shirt damp with sweat, leaning briefly on the doorframe. He's just walked nearly 10km from his longhouse along the hill trail because it's the only way down to the clinic. Dr. Satish stands immediately, helps him to a chair, and offers water.

---

## Shot 3 — The Complaint

Zhu Heng, still catching his breath, describes tightness in his chest that started partway through the walk, plus fatigue and dizziness. Dr. Satish listens, taking notes — his expression shifts to concern. This isn't a routine complaint, and it doesn't fit neatly into anything common he's treated that week.

---

## Shot 4 — No One to Call

Dr. Satish reaches for his phone out of habit — checks it — no signal, as expected out here. He glances at the laptop (also no internet), then at the stack of CPG booklets. He pulls one out and starts flipping, searching for the right section, while Zhu Heng waits, still recovering.

---

## Shot 5 — The Pause

Dr. Satish stops flipping. Looks at Zhu Heng — exhausted from a 10km walk just to get here — then back at the thick, unindexed guideline in his hands. A beat of quiet. He's capable. He's just missing the right tool, at the moment he needs it most.

---

## Text Card (fades in on black)

> *"Long Pa Sia, Sipitang — a 2–3 hour drive from the nearest hospital."*
> *"For Zhu Heng, the clinic itself was a 10km walk."*

---

## Narration (over Shots 3–5)

> "In villages like Long Pa Sia, the clinic is often the only point of care for miles — and reaching it can mean hours of walking. When a patient like Zhu Heng arrives with a case that doesn't fit the routine, Dr. Satish has no specialist to call, no signal to search with — only a guideline too thick to search under pressure."
