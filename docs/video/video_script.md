# ClearPath — 3-Minute Demonstration Video Script

**Total runtime:** ~3:00  
**Format:** Real-life footage (opening) + screen recording (demo) + narration throughout

---

## SEGMENT 1 — Problem Hook [0:00–0:30]
*Real-life footage required*

---

### Scene-by-Scene Shot List

#### Shot 1 — Establishing [0:00–0:05]
**Action:** Wide shot of a small clinic consultation room. A doctor sits alone at a desk. On the desk: a laptop (screen on but idle), a stethoscope, a pen, and a short stack of thick printed CPG documents. The room feels quiet and understaffed — no other staff visible.

**Camera:** Static wide shot from the corner of the room, slightly elevated.

**No text overlay yet.** Let the scene breathe for 2–3 seconds.

---

#### Shot 2 — Patient Enters [0:05–0:10]
**Action:** A knock. The door opens. A patient walks in looking unwell — tired, holding their chest or arm, slightly hesitant. The doctor gestures for them to sit. The doctor picks up a pen, ready to take notes — normal, routine body language.

**Camera:** Medium shot from behind the doctor's shoulder, capturing the patient entering. Hold for 3–4 seconds.

**Text card appears (lower-left, white on dark semi-transparent bar):**
> *"A patient presents with an unusual combination of symptoms."*

---

#### Shot 3 — Doctor Looks Uncertain [0:10–0:17]
**Action:** The patient speaks (no audio needed — can be silent or low ambient sound). The doctor's expression shifts — a slight furrowed brow, leaning back, tapping the pen. The case is clearly uncommon. The doctor glances at the laptop screen and starts typing a search query into a browser. The search results are visibly unhelpful — generic, cluttered. The doctor pauses, unsatisfied.

**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder shot showing both the doctor's face (partial) and the laptop screen. Cut to a close-up of the browser search — results page visible but no clear answer.

**Text card (lower-left):**
> *"No specialist on call. No colleague nearby."*

---

#### Shot 4 — Reaches for the CPG [0:17–0:24]
**Action:** The doctor turns from the laptop and reaches across the desk, pulling one of the thick CPG binders toward them. They flip through it quickly — pages turning fast, fingers scanning dense text and tables, unable to land on the right section. The patient waits in the background, slightly anxious.

**Camera:** Close-up of the hands flipping through the document, then pull back to include the patient waiting in soft focus behind.

**Text card (lower-left):**
> *"Each Clinical Practice Guideline exceeds 100 pages."*

---

#### Shot 5 — Overwhelmed Pause [0:24–0:30]
**Action:** The doctor stops flipping. Sets the document down. Looks at the patient, then back at the screen — a beat of visible uncertainty. A subtle exhale. This is the emotional anchor of the entire opening: a competent, well-meaning clinician, simply without the right tool.

**Camera:** Medium close-up on the doctor's face. Hold for 3 seconds. Then — hard cut to black.

**Text card fades in on black (centred, white text):**
> *"45.6% of rural clinics in Sarawak have no resident doctor."*
> *"Consultations average just 10.5 minutes."*

*(Hold for 1 second before transitioning to Segment 2.)*

---

### Props & Costume Checklist
- [ ] White coat or collared shirt (doctor-appropriate, not overly formal)
- [ ] Stethoscope on desk or around neck
- [ ] 2–3 thick printed documents labelled or resembling CPG booklets (MOH CPG covers are publicly available to print)
- [ ] Laptop open to a browser with a generic medical search query typed in
- [ ] Small clinic desk — plain, slightly cluttered but not chaotic
- [ ] Patient actor: casual clothes, slightly tired/unwell appearance

### Filming Notes
- Shoot Shots 1–5 in sequence but film each shot 2–3 times for editing options
- No dialogue needed — all emotional beats are visual; narration carries the audio
- Ambient sound only (air-con hum, paper rustling, keyboard clicks) — do not use music in this segment
- Total usable footage needed: ~45–60 seconds raw, edited down to 30 seconds

### Narration (voiceover, plays over Shots 3–5)
> "In Malaysia's rural clinics, a single doctor manages the full spectrum of medicine — alone. No specialist on call. No pharmacist. No colleague to consult. When a rare case walks through the door, the only tools available are a browser search and a guideline there's no time to read."

---

## SEGMENT 2 — Problem Statement [0:30–1:00]
*Real-life b-roll footage under narration — 3 beats, each with its own shot*

The narration is split into 3 beats. Each beat has matching background footage and a stat card. No slides needed — the visuals are all live footage with text overlays.

---

### Beat 1 — CPGs Are Too Long to Navigate [0:30–0:41]

**Background footage:**
Doctor at the desk, CPG open flat in front of them. Running a finger slowly down a dense page of text — tables, footnotes, small print. Turns a few pages, finds nothing quickly. Leans back and scratches their head, visibly frustrated. Patient still waiting in soft focus in the background.

**Camera:** Close-up on the document pages first, then pull back to include the doctor's face — tired, not defeated, just stuck.

**Text card (lower-left):**
> *"Each CPG exceeds 100 pages."*
> *"Non-adherence rates reach 39.3%."*

**Narration:**
> "Clinical Practice Guidelines are the standard of care — but each one runs over a hundred pages. In a ten-minute consultation, there's no time to find the right section, let alone read it. So clinicians default to what they remember, or refer the patient out."

---

### Beat 2 — Existing AI Tools Don't Help [0:41–0:52]

**Background footage:**
Same doctor, now turned back to the laptop. Pulls up a ChatGPT-style interface or a generic AI chatbot. Types a clinical question. An answer appears — long, fluent, confident-looking. The doctor reads it, hesitates, scrolls — no citations, no sources, no way to verify. Slight shake of the head or a doubtful expression.

**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder medium shot showing the screen and the doctor's reaction. Cut to a close-up of the screen — the AI response visible but no reference links shown.

**Text card (lower-left):**
> *"General-purpose AI reproduces false clinical facts in up to 83% of adversarial tests."*

**Narration:**
> "Turning to AI doesn't close the gap either. General-purpose models generate fluent, confident answers — with no guarantee they're grounded in local guidelines, and no way to audit the reasoning behind them."

---

### Beat 3 — The Gap Is Structural [0:52–1:00]

**Background footage:**
Wide shot of the full desk scene — laptop open, CPG splayed open beside it, patient sitting across. The doctor looks between the two tools: the screen and the document. Neither is giving them what they need. Hold on this for 3–4 seconds.

**Camera:** Static wide shot, slightly elevated. Let the scene speak. No fast cuts here.

**Text card (centred, larger — this is the thesis statement of the problem):**
> *"The gap isn't the doctor."*
> *"It's the absence of the right tool."*

**Narration:**
> "The problem isn't clinical skill. It's infrastructure. A rural clinician in 2025 deserves the same quality of decision support as a hospital specialist — grounded in evidence, safe to act on, and available in real time."

---

### Props & Filming Notes (Segment 2)
- Reuse the same set and actor from Segment 1 — shoot both segments back to back
- Beat 2 requires the laptop screen to be legible on camera; dim room lighting helps
- For the AI chatbot screen: use a real interface or mock one up — the key visual is a long answer with no citations visible
- Beat 3 is a held wide shot — resist the urge to cut; the stillness is intentional
- All three beats can be filmed in 20–30 minutes if Segment 1 is already set up

---

## SEGMENT 3 — System Introduction [1:00–1:15]
*Screen recording — ClearPath UI landing page*

**Main focus:** The ClearPath UI opens on screen. Let it load naturally. The interface itself is the introduction.

**Brief overlay card (bottom-left corner, small, fades in after 3 seconds):**
> `ClearPath — Evidence-Based Clinical Practice Guidance System`

**Narration (plays over the UI):**
> "ClearPath is a clinical decision support system built for Malaysia's rural front line. Every recommendation is grounded in Ministry of Health Clinical Practice Guidelines, every reasoning step is visible, and every medication is checked before sign-off."

---

## SEGMENT 4 — Live System Demo [1:15–2:30]
*Screen recording of the ClearPath UI — 4 scenes, continuous flow*

**General screen recording instructions:**
- Record at 1080p minimum, 60fps preferred
- Use one real demo case throughout all 4 scenes: *58-year-old male, T2DM + hypertension, chest tightness on exertion, currently on metformin 500mg + amlodipine 5mg*
- Slow, deliberate mouse movements — no fast scrolling
- Cards are small overlays only (bottom-left or bottom-right corner) — they must never cover the UI content
- Zoom-punch into key UI elements in post if needed (CapCut / Premiere)

---

### Scene 4a — Query Input & Routing [1:15–1:35]

**Main focus — screen:**
1. Cursor moves to the case input field
2. Case typed in (or typing animation): *"58M, T2DM and hypertension, presents with chest tightness on exertion, on metformin 500mg and amlodipine 5mg. What are the relevant CPG recommendations?"*
3. Submit clicked — brief loading indicator
4. Routing result appears on screen:
   - CPG matched: **CPG Management of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (6th Ed)**
   - ICD-11: **5A11**
   - Status: **Within validated scope**

**Brief overlay card (bottom-left, small):**
> `Layer 1 — Query Router: CPG matched · Scope confirmed`

**Narration:**
> "The clinician describes the case. Before retrieving anything, the routing layer identifies the governing CPG, maps the ICD-11 code, and confirms scope. If a case falls outside validated guidelines — ClearPath says so, rather than guessing."

---

### Scene 4b — Guideline-Grounded Answer [1:35–1:55]

**Main focus — screen:**
1. Screen scrolls to the retrieval results — numbered recommendations, each with a citation tag e.g. `[CPG DM §4.2]`
2. Cursor hovers a citation — tooltip or side panel opens showing the exact CPG passage it was drawn from
3. Hold 2–3 seconds on the open citation panel so the source link is clearly visible

**Brief overlay card (bottom-left, small):**
> `Layer 2 — Hybrid RAG: CPG DM §4.2, §6.1 · Source-cited`

**Narration:**
> "Every recommendation is pulled only from the governing guideline — not general training data. Each one is cited at section level. The clinician can open any citation and read the exact passage it came from."

---

### Scene 4c — Medication Safety Critic [1:55–2:15]

**Main focus — screen:**
1. Screen scrolls to the medication safety section — patient's current drugs listed: metformin + amlodipine
2. Proposed addition visible in the plan: ramipril
3. Red warning banner appears — this is the visual centrepiece of the scene, let it sit:
   - `⚠ SAFETY FLAG — Potential interaction detected`
   - `Ramipril: eGFR confirmation required before prescribing`
   - `Source: CPG Hypertension §8.3 · Drug Knowledge Graph`
4. "Confirm Care Plan" button is greyed out — blocked
5. Doctor clicks "Acknowledge & Document" — button turns green, plan unlocks

**Brief overlay card (bottom-left, small):**
> `Layer 3 — Safety Critic: Flag raised · Sign-off required`

**Narration:**
> "Before the plan is finalised, a dual-source safety critic checks every medication — combining LLM pharmacological reasoning with a structured drug knowledge graph. A flag blocks sign-off until the clinician acknowledges it. No silent failures."

---

### Scene 4d — Structured Care Plan Output [2:15–2:30]

**Main focus — screen:**
1. Final care plan renders in full — structured sections clearly visible:
   - **Diagnosis:** T2DM with hypertension
   - **Investigations:** HbA1c, eGFR, lipid panel
   - **Treatment:** Updated medication list with dosing
   - **Follow-up:** 3-month review, red flags
2. Banner visible at bottom: *Saved to patient record*
3. Quick cut: same patient, new visit — previous care plan pre-loaded as context

**Brief overlay card (bottom-left, small):**
> `Layer 4 — Care Plan: Structured · Saved · Carried across visits`

**Narration:**
> "The output is a structured, executable care plan — not a paragraph to re-read under time pressure. It's saved to the patient record and pre-loaded at the next visit, so every consultation builds on the last."

---

## SEGMENT 5 — Unique Value + Closing [2:30–3:00]
*Screen recording continues — capability comparison visible in UI or shown as a clean overlay graphic*

**Main focus — screen:**
The capability gap table animates on screen — rows appear one at a time. The first three columns (GPT-4o, NotebookLM, Qmed AskCPG) fill with ✗ marks, then the ClearPath column fills last with ✓ marks one by one. Hold on the completed table for 3–4 seconds.

| Capability | GPT-4o | NotebookLM | Qmed AskCPG | ClearPath |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Grounded in Malaysian MOH CPGs | ✗ | partial | ✓ | ✓ |
| Declines out-of-scope cases | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Independent medication-safety check | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Structured, actionable care plan | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Auditable reasoning trace | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Continuity across patient visits | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

After the table, screen fades to the ClearPath landing page one last time — clean, simple.

**Brief overlay card (bottom-centre, slightly larger for the closing):**
> `ClearPath — Evidence-based guidance, at the point of care.`
> `[Team names] · [Institution]`

**Narration:**
> "ClearPath closes every structural gap that existing tools cannot — not by using a larger model, but by building reliability into the architecture itself. Grounded retrieval, scope enforcement, an independent safety layer, and a structured output that works inside the consultation, not around it."

**Final beat (last 5 seconds — no narration, screen only):**
ClearPath UI on screen, idle. Fade to black. Hold.

## Production Notes

### Real-life filming checklist (Segment 1 only)
- [ ] Clinic desk with visible CPG binders or thick printed documents
- [ ] Patient queue or waiting area visible
- [ ] Neutral/natural lighting — avoid overly staged look
- [ ] 15–20 seconds of usable b-roll is enough; the segment is 30 seconds total
- [ ] Optional prop: printed CPG document opened to a dense page of text

### Screen recording checklist (Segments 3–5)
- [ ] Record at 1080p or higher
- [ ] Use a real or realistic demo case (e.g., hypertension + diabetes + polypharmacy)
- [ ] Slow cursor movements and hover to guide viewer's eye
- [ ] Callout labels can be added in post (DaVinci Resolve / CapCut / Premiere)
- [ ] Avoid recording with personal data visible

### Audio
- [ ] Record narration separately in a quiet room; sync to footage in post
- [ ] Background music: low, ambient — must not compete with voiceover
- [ ] Narration pace: ~130 words/minute (each segment timed above matches this)

## Shooting Day Plan

Two separate sessions — keep real-life filming and screen recording on different days so neither blocks the other.

---

### Session 1 — Real-Life Filming (Segments 1 & 2)
**Estimated time: 2–3 hours including setup**
**People needed: 2 (1 actor as doctor, 1 as patient) + 1 person operating camera**

#### Pre-shoot prep (do this before the day)
- [ ] Print 2–3 CPG covers and staple thick stacks of paper inside to look like real booklets — MOH CPG covers are available online
- [ ] Prepare the laptop with a browser open to a generic medical search (e.g. Google with a clinical query typed in but not submitted), and a ChatGPT-style interface open in another tab with a long answer visible but no citation links
- [ ] Identify a room that can pass as a small clinic consultation room — a plain desk, two chairs facing each other, neutral wall behind. A university meeting room or small office works.
- [ ] Doctor costume: white coat or collared shirt, stethoscope on desk or around neck
- [ ] Patient costume: casual clothes, slightly tired appearance
- [ ] Camera: phone on a tripod is fine — stabilised, 1080p, landscape orientation

#### On the day — shoot order

**Block A — Wide and establishing shots (15 min)**
Shoot these first while the set is clean and actors are fresh.
1. Shot 1 (Seg 1): Wide establishing shot — doctor at desk alone, laptop + CPG stack visible. Film 3 takes.
2. Beat 3 (Seg 2): Wide held shot — doctor looking between laptop and document. Film 3 takes. *(Shoot this now while the wide angle is already set up — even though it appears later in the video)*

**Block B — Patient entry and reaction shots (30 min)**
3. Shot 2 (Seg 1): Patient knocks and enters, doctor gestures to sit. Film 3 takes from behind-doctor angle.
4. Shot 3 (Seg 1): Doctor looks uncertain, turns to laptop, types search query, reacts to unhelpful results. Film 3 takes — over-the-shoulder + close-up of screen separately.
5. Beat 2 (Seg 2): Doctor tries the AI chatbot, reads the answer, looks doubtful. Film 3 takes — reuse the same laptop tab. *(Efficient: same setup as Shot 3)*

**Block C — CPG close-up shots (20 min)**
6. Shot 4 (Seg 1): Close-up of hands flipping through CPG, patient waiting in background soft focus. Film 3 takes.
7. Beat 1 (Seg 2): Doctor reading CPG slowly, finger scanning page, scratches head. Film 3 takes — mix close-up of document and doctor's face.

**Block D — Emotional anchor shots (15 min)**
8. Shot 5 (Seg 1): Doctor stops, sets document down, pauses — the overwhelmed beat. Film 5 takes — this is the most important shot, get a clean one.

**Block E — Pickup shots (20 min)**
Any missed angles, reaction cutaways, or b-roll that wasn't captured cleanly. Common pickups:
- Close-up of the laptop search results page
- Doctor's face during patient explanation (nodding, then frowning)
- Patient's anxious waiting expression
- Hands on keyboard

---

### Session 2 — Screen Recording (Segments 3, 4, 5)
**Estimated time: 1–2 hours**
**People needed: 1 (whoever knows the system best)**

#### Pre-recording prep (do this before the session)
- [ ] Have the demo case ready to type: *"58M, T2DM and hypertension, presents with chest tightness on exertion, on metformin 500mg and amlodipine 5mg. What are the relevant CPG recommendations?"*
- [ ] Confirm the system is running and all 4 layers produce clean output for this case
- [ ] Close all unnecessary browser tabs and notifications — put the machine on Do Not Disturb
- [ ] Set screen resolution to 1920×1080, recording software open (OBS / Loom / built-in)
- [ ] Prepare the capability comparison table as a separate graphic (Canva or PowerPoint) for Segment 5

#### On the day — record order

**Block A — Full run-through (30 min)**
Do one complete uninterrupted recording of the entire demo flow — Segments 3 through 4d. This gives you a master take to fall back on even if individual scenes have issues.

1. Start at the ClearPath landing page (Seg 3)
2. Type the demo case slowly, submit (Seg 4a)
3. Scroll to routing result, pause 3 seconds (Seg 4a)
4. Scroll to retrieval results, hover a citation, pause 3 seconds (Seg 4b)
5. Scroll to medication safety section, let the flag appear, pause 3 seconds (Seg 4c)
6. Click Acknowledge, let the care plan render, pause 3 seconds (Seg 4d)
7. Show the "saved to patient record" state (Seg 4d)

**Block B — Isolated scene re-records (30 min)**
Re-record each scene individually so you have clean isolated clips for editing. Easier to sync narration when each scene is its own file.
- [ ] Seg 3 alone: landing page, 15 seconds
- [ ] Seg 4a alone: input → routing result
- [ ] Seg 4b alone: scroll to results → hover citation → hold
- [ ] Seg 4c alone: safety flag → acknowledge → unlock
- [ ] Seg 4d alone: care plan render → saved banner → second visit pre-load

**Block C — Capability table recording (15 min)**
- [ ] Record the animated capability table graphic (Seg 5) — rows appearing one at a time
- [ ] End with a final recording of the ClearPath landing page idle for 5 seconds (closing fade)

---

### Session 3 — Narration Recording
**Estimated time: 45 min**
**People needed: 1 narrator, 1 to manage recording**

- Record in a quiet room — closet, padded room, or room with soft furnishings
- Use any decent microphone — phone with a lapel mic is fine, avoid built-in laptop mic
- Read each segment's narration as a separate audio file — easier to sync in post
- Record each line 2–3 times; pick the best take in editing
- Speak at ~130 words/minute — conversational, calm, not rushed

- [ ] `narration_seg2_beat3.mp3` — Beat 3 voiceover
- [ ] `narration_seg3.mp3` — System introduction

### Post-Production Order (editing sequence)
1. Sync narration audio files to their matching video segments first
2. Trim real-life footage (Seg 1 & 2) to match narration length
3. Add text overlay cards — keep them small, bottom-corner, consistent font
4. Assemble all segments in order on the timeline
5. Add subtle ambient background music (low volume, fades under narration)
6. Export at 1080p, H.264, for YouTube / submission upload
