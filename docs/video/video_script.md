## SEGMENT 1 — Problem Hook [0:00–0:40]
*Real-life footage — merges the hook and problem statement into one continuous scene*

---

### Shot 1 — Establishing [0:00–0:05]
**Action:** Wide shot of a small clinic consultation room. Doctor sits alone at a desk — laptop open, stethoscope, a stack of thick CPG binders. Room feels quiet and understaffed.

**Camera:** Static wide shot, slightly elevated. No text overlay. Let it breathe.

---

### Shot 2 — Patient Enters [0:05–0:10]
**Action:** A knock. Patient walks in looking unwell. Doctor gestures to sit, picks up a pen.

**Camera:** Medium shot from behind the doctor's shoulder.

**Text card (lower-left):**
> *"A patient presents with an unusual combination of symptoms."*

---

### Shot 3 — Doctor Searches, Fails [0:10–0:20]
**Action:** Doctor types into a browser — results are cluttered, unhelpful. Turns to a CPG binder, flips pages fast, can't find the right section. Patient waits in background.

**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder on the laptop screen, then close-up of hands flipping the CPG.

**Text card (lower-left):**
> *"Each CPG exceeds 100 pages."*
> *"General-purpose AI has no guarantee of clinical accuracy."*

**Narration:**
> "In Malaysia's rural clinics, a single doctor manages the full spectrum of medicine — alone. No specialist on call. When a complex case walks in, the only tools available are a browser search and a guideline there's no time to read."

---

### Shot 4 — The Pause [0:20–0:25]
**Action:** Doctor stops. Sets the document down. Looks at the patient — a beat of visible uncertainty. Hard cut to black.

**Camera:** Medium close-up on the doctor's face. Hold 3 seconds, then cut.

---

### Shot 5 — Stats on Black [0:25–0:40]
**Action:** Black screen. Two stat cards fade in one at a time, then the ClearPath logo reveals.

**Text cards (centred, white on black):**
> *"45.6% of rural clinics in Sarawak have no resident doctor."*
> *"CPG non-adherence rates reach 39.3%."*
> *(pause 1 second)*
> **ClearPath.**

**Narration:**
> "The problem isn't the doctor. It's the absence of the right tool. ClearPath is that tool."

## Shooting Day Plan

Two separate sessions — keep real-life filming and screen recording on different days so neither blocks the other.

---

### Session 1 — Real-Life Filming (Segments 1 & 2)
**Estimated time: 2–3 hours including setup**
**People needed: 2 (1 actor as doctor, 1 as patient) + 1 person operating camera**

#### Pre-shoot prep (do this before the day)
- [ ] Print 2–3 CPG covers and staple thick stacks of paper inside to look like real booklets — MOH CPG covers are available online
- [ ] Prepare the laptop with a browser open to a generic medical search (e.g. Google with a clinical query typed in but not submitted), and a ChatGPT-style interface open in another tab with a long answer visible but no citation links
- [ ] Identify a room that can pass as a small clinic consultation room — a plain desk, two chairs facing each other, neutral wall behind. A university meeting room or small office works.
- [ ] Doctor costume: white coat or collared shirt, stethoscope on desk or around neck
- [ ] Patient costume: casual clothes, slightly tired appearance
- [ ] Camera: phone on a tripod is fine — stabilised, 1080p, landscape orientation

#### On the day — shoot order

**Block A — Wide and establishing shots (15 min)**
Shoot these first while the set is clean and actors are fresh.
1. Shot 1 (Seg 1): Wide establishing shot — doctor at desk alone, laptop + CPG stack visible. Film 3 takes.
2. Beat 3 (Seg 2): Wide held shot — doctor looking between laptop and document. Film 3 takes. *(Shoot this now while the wide angle is already set up — even though it appears later in the video)*

**Block B — Patient entry and reaction shots (30 min)**
3. Shot 2 (Seg 1): Patient knocks and enters, doctor gestures to sit. Film 3 takes from behind-doctor angle.
4. Shot 3 (Seg 1): Doctor looks uncertain, turns to laptop, types search query, reacts to unhelpful results. Film 3 takes — over-the-shoulder + close-up of screen separately.
5. Beat 2 (Seg 2): Doctor tries the AI chatbot, reads the answer, looks doubtful. Film 3 takes — reuse the same laptop tab. *(Efficient: same setup as Shot 3)*

**Block C — CPG close-up shots (20 min)**
6. Shot 4 (Seg 1): Close-up of hands flipping through CPG, patient waiting in background soft focus. Film 3 takes.
7. Beat 1 (Seg 2): Doctor reading CPG slowly, finger scanning page, scratches head. Film 3 takes — mix close-up of document and doctor's face.

**Block D — Emotional anchor shots (15 min)**
8. Shot 5 (Seg 1): Doctor stops, sets document down, pauses — the overwhelmed beat. Film 5 takes — this is the most important shot, get a clean one.

**Block E — Pickup shots (20 min)**
Any missed angles, reaction cutaways, or b-roll that wasn't captured cleanly. Common pickups:
- Close-up of the laptop search results page
- Doctor's face during patient explanation (nodding, then frowning)
- Patient's anxious waiting expression
- Hands on keyboard

6. Export at 1080p, H.264, for YouTube / submission upload
