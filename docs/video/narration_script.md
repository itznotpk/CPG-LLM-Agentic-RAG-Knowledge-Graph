# ClearPath — Narration Script (Speaker-Ready)
**Total runtime: ~3:00 | Pace: ~130 wpm | Record each segment as a separate audio file**

## SEGMENT 1 — Problem Hook [0:00–0:30]
*Voiceover begins at Shot 3 (doctor turns to laptop). Silence over Shots 1–2.*

> "In Long Pa Sia, deep in the highlands of Sipitang, Dr. Satish is the only doctor for miles.
> Today, a patient — Zhu Heng — walks in after a ten-kilometre trek down the hill trail,
> exhausted, with chest tightness that doesn't fit anything routine.
> No specialist on call. No signal to search with.
> Just a guideline too thick to search under pressure."

*(Hard cut to black. Text card holds on screen: "Long Pa Sia, Sipitang — a 2–3 hour drive from the nearest hospital. For Zhu Heng, the clinic itself was a 10km walk." No narration over this card — let it sit.)*

## SEGMENT 2 — Problem Statement [0:30–1:00]

**Beat 1 — Clinical Decision Isolation** *(doctor scanning CPG pages):*
> "In Malaysia's primary care settings, clinicians face complex cases without real-time diagnostic support.
> Clinical Practice Guidelines exist — but they're static, hundred-page documents
> with no way to query them against a specific patient in front of you."

**Beat 2 — Ungoverned Retrieval** *(doctor reading AI chatbot response with no citations):*
> "Existing AI tools aren't tailored to local guidelines — they're generic.
> Responses are drawn from global datasets with no guarantee they reflect Malaysian CPG standards,
> and with a real risk of hallucination."

**Beat 3 — Opaque Reasoning + Non-Executable Outputs** *(held wide shot — doctor between laptop and CPG):*
> "And even when AI produces an answer, there's no way to verify it.
> No traceable evidence. No citations.
> Just unstructured text the clinician can't act on directly —
> and certainly can't put in a patient record.
> The gap isn't the doctor. It's the absence of the right tool."

## SEGMENT 3 — System Introduction [1:00–1:50]
*Screen recording begins. One continuous take.*

**Scene 3a — Landing Page** *(8s)*
> "This is ClearPath —
> a clinical decision support system built on Malaysia's Ministry of Health guidelines.
> Every decision it makes is grounded in evidence, auditable, and patient-specific."

**Scene 3b — Sign In** *(10s)*
> "Access is role-gated.
> Each clinician logs in to their own workspace —
> consultation history, patients, and performance metrics are all personal to them."

**Scene 3c — Main Dashboard** *(12s)*
> "The home screen gives the clinician their full day —
> who's waiting, who's in queue,
> and a live tally of how the AI assistant has impacted their practice today:
> time saved, CPG alignment rate, and evidence-backed citations."

**Scene 3d — Patient Dashboard** *(15s)*
> "Every patient's record is persistent.
> Comorbidities, current medications, allergy flags, and consultation history are all pre-loaded —
> so the clinician walks in already informed, not starting from scratch."

**Scene 3e — Start Consultation** *(10s)*
> "Starting a consultation takes one step —
> enter the patient's IC number.
> The system retrieves their full record instantly."

**Scene 3f — Clinical Assessment Form** *(10s)*
> "The assessment form captures everything in one place.
> Patient context is pre-filled from their record.
> Vitals are entered manually —
> or captured in seconds with two built-in tools that keep the workflow seamless."

**Scene 3g — rPPG + STT Callout** *(5s)*
> "The rPPG scanner reads heart rate and oxygen saturation from the camera — no contact required.
> Speech-to-text transcribes the clinician's dictation directly into structured clinical notes.
> Both tools eliminate manual entry so the doctor stays focused on the patient."

## SEGMENT 4 — Agentic Pipeline [1:50–2:40]
*Screen recording continues without cut.*

**Scene 4a — AI Reasoning Trace Loading** *(10s)*
> "The moment the assessment is submitted, ClearPath begins a four-stage agentic pipeline —
> differential diagnosis, CPG routing, evidence retrieval, and plan synthesis.
> Every stage is visible in real time.
> The clinician is never waiting on a black box."

**Scene 4b — DDx + ICD-11 Mapping** *(25s)*
> "The differential diagnosis layer extracts symptoms from the clinical notes,
> maps them to ICD-11 codes, and ranks each candidate by retrieval confidence.
> The clinician sees the AI's reasoning —
> extracted symptom queries, hypothesis generation, code injection logic —
> not just a final answer.
> They confirm or override each diagnosis before anything proceeds."

**Scene 4c — CPG Routing** *(15s)*
> "CPG Routing maps each confirmed diagnosis to its governing Ministry of Health guideline.
> The system validates ICD codes, resolves parent hierarchies,
> and automatically excludes guidelines that don't apply —
> for example, a female-only guideline is excluded for a male patient.
> Only relevant, matched guidelines proceed."

**Scene 4d — Evidence Retrieval** *(10s)*
> "Evidence retrieval fires targeted queries against the matched CPGs —
> not a single broad search, but a structured retrieval strategy.
> Each chunk is scored for cosine similarity.
> The mean match score and retrieval quality index are shown live."

**Scene 4e — Care Plan + Safety Review** *(12s)*
> "The care plan arrives structured into five sections —
> a clinical summary, medication changes with evidence grades,
> care instructions, red flags, and a follow-up timeline.
> But before the clinician can approve anything,
> the safety layer has already flagged a critical concern:
> PDE-5 inhibitors combined with nitrates risk severe hypotension.
> The system blocks sign-off until the clinician explicitly acknowledges it.
> The plan cannot be silently accepted."

**Scene 4f — Final Care Plan + Approval** *(8s)*
> "The final output is a SOAP-structured clinical document —
> complete with vitals, diagnoses, medications, and follow-up plan.
> One click approves and saves it to the patient record.
> The entire consultation, from data input to approved care plan, is fully auditable."

## SEGMENT 5 — Key Features Highlight [2:40–3:15]
*Quick-cut montage — re-cuts of footage from Segments 3–4, paired with icon/label cards.*

> "Beyond the consultation flow, ClearPath brings a full toolkit to the point of care.
> Contactless rPPG reads heart rate and oxygen levels from the camera.
> Voice dictation turns spoken notes into structured records.
> The ICD-11 engine ranks differential diagnoses across 248 codes.
> Every recommendation is grounded and cited from 30 Malaysian CPGs.
> An independent safety critic checks medications before sign-off.
> And every care plan is structured, audit-logged, and ready to export."

## SEGMENT 6 — Closing [3:15–3:35]
*Comparison table on screen. Narration plays over it, then fades to ClearPath landing page.*

> "Remember that doctor —
> alone, with no specialist on call, and a guideline too long to read in time?
> ClearPath puts specialist-level guidance in their hands in seconds:
> grounded in the right CPG section,
> checked by an independent safety critic,
> and delivered as a structured plan they can act on immediately.
> The gap wasn't the doctor.
> ClearPath is the tool."

*(Final 5 seconds: ClearPath landing page, no narration. Fade to black.)*

## Recording Tips

- Read each segment label aloud first to orient yourself — then pause 2 seconds before speaking the narration
- **3g and 4f are short** — slow down, don't rush to fill time
- **4e is the centrepiece** — pause naturally before "The system blocks sign-off..." to let the safety flag moment land
- **Segment 5 (Key Features Highlight) is fast-paced** — read it slightly more energetically than the rest; each feature gets ~5–6 seconds, don't linger
- Record every segment 2–3 times; keep the take that feels most conversational, not the most polished
- Save files as: `narration_seg1.mp3`, `narration_seg2_beat1.mp3`, `narration_seg2_beat2.mp3`, `narration_seg2_beat3.mp3`, `narration_seg3a.mp3` … `narration_seg5.mp3`, `narration_seg6.mp3`
