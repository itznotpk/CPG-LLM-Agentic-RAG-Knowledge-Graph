# 🎬 ClearPath — 3-Minute Showcase Video Plan

## Overview

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Total Duration** | 3:00 |
| **Tone** | Professional yet energetic — startup pitch meets clinical credibility |
| **Format** | Face-to-camera narration + live demo screen recording + text overlays |

---

## 🎯 Recording Setup

### Equipment
- **Camera:** Phone on tripod (1080p minimum), or laptop webcam for face shots
- **Screen Recording:** OBS Studio (free) or built-in screen recorder for demo sections
- **Audio:** Use a quiet room. A clip-on lapel mic makes a huge difference. If unavailable, record in a small quiet room and speak clearly
- **Lighting:** Face a window for natural light, or use a desk lamp pointed at your face (not behind you)

### Setting Options (Pick One)

| Setting | Vibe | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Clean desk setup** | Professional, focused | Laptop open, plain wall behind, good lighting |
| **Clinic-style background** | Clinical credibility | White wall, stethoscope/medical book as prop |
| **Split-screen studio** | Tech-forward | Speaker on one side, screen demo on the other (edit in post) |

> **💡 Tip:** Wear a plain, solid-color shirt (no stripes/patterns). It looks clean on camera.

## 🎞️ Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1 — The Hook (0:00 – 0:20) ⏱️ 20s

**Visual:** Face-to-camera OR dramatic text animation  
**Setting:** Speaker looking directly into camera

**Script idea:**
> *"A doctor in rural Sabah sees a diabetic patient with kidney disease. She opens a 200-page PDF guideline to find the right drug. By the time she finds it — the patient has left. This is the reality of clinical practice in Malaysia."*

**Creative touch:**
- Start with a 3-second **black screen** with the sound of pages flipping, then cut to speaker
- OR show a quick montage: PDF → scrolling → confused face → ClearPath logo reveal

> **⚠️ Important:** The first 5 seconds decide if people keep watching. Start with the **problem**, not the product name.

---

### Scene 2 — The Problem (0:20 – 0:50) ⏱️ 30s

**Visual:** Face-to-camera with text overlays / icons appearing beside speaker  
**Setting:** Same as Scene 1

**Key points to hit (pick 3–4):**

| Icon | Point |
|------|-------|
| 📄 | Malaysia's CPGs are **200+ page static PDFs** — not searchable, not patient-aware |
| 🤥 | Gen AI tools **hallucinate** — dangerous in healthcare |
| 📋 | NotebookLM can summarise but **never makes clinical decisions** |
| 🔍 | Existing CPG search tools **can't reason about a specific patient** |
| 🏥 | Rural primary care doctors are **isolated** with no specialist access |

**Script idea:**
> *"Malaysia's Ministry of Health publishes Clinical Practice Guidelines — but they're locked in static PDFs. Gen AI hallucinates. NotebookLM summarises but never decides. No tool today can look at YOUR patient and tell you the right next step — safely."*

**Creative touch:**
- Flash each problem as a bold text overlay with a ❌ appearing next to it
- Quick 2-second screen recording showing a real CPG PDF being scrolled (overwhelming)

---

### Scene 3 — The Solution: ClearPath (0:50 – 1:20) ⏱️ 30s

**Visual:** Logo reveal → transition to architecture/system overview  
**Setting:** Cut to a clean slide or animated graphic

**Script idea:**
> *"ClearPath transforms these static guidelines into a real-time, patient-aware routing engine."*

**Key points to hit:**

| Icon | Feature | One-liner |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 🔄 | **Real-Time CPG Engine** | Converts static PDFs into a live knowledge graph |
| 📊 | **Evidence-Graded** | Every recommendation shows its evidence level and source |
| 🛡️ | **Safety-Critic Agent** | An adversarial AI agent that challenges every recommendation |
| 🧠 | **Patient-Aware** | Understands the patient as a structured object, not just text |
| 🚫 | **Refuses Unsafe Plans** | Won't sign off on care plans that fail safety checks |

**Creative touch:**
- Show a simple **architecture diagram** (even hand-drawn on whiteboard looks great)
- Animate each feature appearing one by one with icons
- OR use a Canva/PowerPoint slide with the system flow:
  ```
  Patient Data → RAG + Knowledge Graph → Safety Agent → Doctor Dashboard
  ```

---

### Scene 4 — Live Demo (1:20 – 2:30) ⏱️ 70s ⭐ THE STAR

> **⚠️ This is the most important section. Show, don't tell. The demo must be smooth and rehearsed.**

**Visual:** Full-screen recording of the Doctor UI  
**Setting:** Screen recording with voiceover narration

**Demo Flow:**

| Step | Duration | What to Show | What to Say |
|------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **1. Enter patient** | 15s | Input a realistic patient case | *"Let's say we have a 55-year-old diabetic patient with Stage 3 CKD..."* |
| **2. AI processing** | 15s | System querying knowledge graph, retrieving CPG recommendations | *"ClearPath retrieves relevant guidelines from Malaysia's CPGs, graded by evidence level..."* |
| **3. Diagnosis** | 15s | Diagnosis section, treatment recommendations with evidence grades | *"Each recommendation is traceable — you can see exactly which guideline page it came from..."* |
| **4. Safety review** | 15s | Safety-Critic Agent reviewing, flagging, approving | *"Now the safety-critic agent reviews the entire plan. It challenges drug interactions, checks contraindications..."* |
| **5. Approved plan** | 10s | Final approved care plan with safety badge | *"Only after passing safety review does the doctor see the final recommendation. No hallucinations. No guesswork."* |

**Creative touches:**
- Use **zoom-in** on key UI elements (evidence badges, safety review banner)
- Add subtle **highlight boxes** around important features in post-editing
- Use a **real patient scenario** that's relatable (e.g., diabetic patient in rural clinic)
- If possible, show a **failure case** — where the safety agent **rejects** an unsafe plan (powerful and memorable)

> **💡 Tip:** Rehearse the demo **3–5 times** before recording. Pre-load the patient data so there's no typing delay.

---

### Scene 5 — Impact & Closing (2:30 – 3:00) ⏱️ 30s

**Visual:** Back to face-to-camera → end with team photo / logo  
**Setting:** Same as Scene 1

**Script idea:**
> *"ClearPath isn't just another AI tool. It's a safety-first clinical decision engine built specifically for Malaysia's healthcare system. For the rural doctor who doesn't have a specialist down the hall — ClearPath is that specialist. Thank you."*

**Key closing points:**
- 🇲🇾 Built specifically for **Malaysia's CPGs**
- 🛡️ **Safety-first** — the only system that actively refuses unsafe plans
- 🏥 Designed for **primary care** where it's needed most

**Creative touch:**
- End with the ClearPath logo + tagline on screen for 3 seconds
- Suggested tagline: *"Evidence-graded. Safety-vetted. Patient-first."*
- Optional: Show team names/photos in the last 5 seconds

## 🎨 Post-Production

### Editing Tools (Free)

| Tool | Platform | Best For |
|------|----------|----------|
| **CapCut** | Desktop / Mobile | Text overlays, transitions, captions |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Desktop | Professional editing, color grading |
| **Canva Video** | Web | Animated slides, easy templates |

### Must-Do Edits
- [ ] Add **subtitles/captions** — many viewers watch without sound
- [ ] Add **background music** — royalty-free lo-fi or corporate track (low volume)
- [ ] Add **text overlays** for key terms (e.g., "Safety-Critic Agent" appears on screen when mentioned)
- [ ] **Smooth transitions** between face-cam and screen recording (simple fade or cut)
- [ ] Add **zoom effects** on screen recording to highlight key UI elements

- Pixabay Music (free)

- [ ] Recording environment is **quiet and well-lit**
- [ ] Phone/camera is **stable** (tripod or propped against books)
- [ ] Screen recording software **tested** (OBS / built-in recorder)
- [ ] Dress code: **clean, solid-color shirt**
- [ ] All notifications **turned off** on demo machine
- [ ] Browser bookmarks bar **hidden** during screen recording
- [ ] Demo uses a **realistic patient scenario** (not "John Doe, age 30")
