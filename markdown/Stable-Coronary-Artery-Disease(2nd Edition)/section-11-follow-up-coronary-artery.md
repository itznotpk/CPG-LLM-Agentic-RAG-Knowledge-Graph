## 11: Follow-Up Of Patients With Stable Cad
There are no randomized trials evaluating the impact on outcome of different strategies for the follow-up of patients with stable CAD. In addition, there are currently no data suggesting that any form of follow up stress testing improves outcome in asymptomatic patients.527

The frequency of follow-up and further evaluation of these patients will depend on:
- severity of CAD
- comorbidities and optimization of risk factors
- local resources

Clinical judgement is required in determining the need for repeated testing.

## Key Messages:
- All patients with Stable CAD can be managed both at hospital and at general outpatient clinics.
- In general, a repeat exercise stress ECG (or pharmacological stress) may be warranted if there is a change in the patient's:
  - symptoms - worsening angina or effort tolerance
  - and/or heart failure
  - development of malignant arrhythmias
- Whenever indicated, these patients should be referred to tertiary cardiac centres for optimization of management. When stable, they can be transferred back to general outpatient clinics with Family Medicine Specialists.
