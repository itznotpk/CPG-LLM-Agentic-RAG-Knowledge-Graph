## 3.7 Treatment Algorithms for the Management of T2DM

### Algorithm 1: Treatment Algorithm for Newly Diagnosed T2DM

All newly diagnosed T2DM patients should receive **lifestyle modification** (diet, exercise, weight reduction) at every juncture of treatment. The following treatment steps are based on HbA1c and FPG at the time of diagnosis:

---

**STEP 1 — Assessment of HbA1c and FPG at Diagnosis**

| HbA1c / FPG at Diagnosis | Initial Treatment |
|---|---|
| **HbA1c <6.5% AND FPG <6.0 mmol/L** | Consider metformin monotherapy^b^ |
| **HbA1c 6.5–7.4% OR FPG 6.0–7.9 mmol/L** | Mono- or dual-therapy^c^ with: Metformin^d^ OR SU, DPP4-i, GLP1-RA, SGLT2-i |
| **HbA1c 7.5–8.4% OR FPG 8.0–9.9 mmol/L** | Dual combination therapy^e^ with any combination of: Metformin, SU, Meglitinide, AGI, TZD, DPP4-i, GLP1-RA, SGLT2-i, Insulin |
| **HbA1c 8.5–10.0% OR FPG 10.0–13.0 mmol/L** | Triple combination therapy^e^ with any combination of: Metformin, SU, Meglitinide, AGI, TZD, DPP4-i, GLP1-RA, SGLT2-i, Insulin |
| **HbA1c >10.0% OR FPG >13.0 mmol/L**^f^ | Basal/premixed insulin therapy + Combination therapy, OR Intensive insulin therapy + OGLD |

*^b^ If patients are able to normalise plasma glucose (glucose profile/HbA1c) and/or lose weight, may continue metformin.*
*^c^ Consider addition of DPP4-i (vildagliptin) — In the VERIFY trial metformin + vildagliptin delays loss of glycaemic control when initiated within 24 months of diagnosis.*
*^d^ Meglitinide/AGI/TZD may not be appropriate for monotherapy.*
*^e^ Combination therapy includes both oral and injectable (GLP1-RA) GLD.*
*^f^ For patients who are symptomatic, initiate insulin directly.*

**Drug Colour Code for Efficacy and Weight/Hypoglycaemia Profile:**
- **Dark blue** (Efficacious, low risk of hypoglycaemia and weight neutral): DPP4-i, SGLT2-i-type agents
- **Medium blue** (Efficacious, risk of hypoglycaemia and weight gain): Insulin
- **Light blue** (Efficacious, low risk of hypoglycaemia and weight loss): GLP1-RA, SGLT2-i
- **Grey** (Moderate efficacy, low risk of hypoglycaemia and weight neutral): AGI
- **Darker grey** (Moderate efficacy, low risk of hypoglycaemia and weight gain): TZD

**STEP 2 — Follow-up After 3–6 Months**
- Optimise dose of GLD agent in subsequent 3–6 months.
- Follow-up with HbA1c after 3–6 months.
- If HbA1c ≤6.5%, continue therapy.
- If HbA1c >6.5%, escalate to next tier.

---

### Algorithm 2: Treatment Recommendations for T2DM on Clinic Follow-up

At each 3–6-month follow-up, if **HbA1c is above individualised target** despite lifestyle modification + existing therapy, escalate treatment based on the degree of HbA1c elevation:

| HbA1c above individualised target | Action |
|---|---|
| **0.5%–1% above target** | Add 1 additional GLD from different class, OR Intensification of insulin (if already on insulin) |
| **1%–2% above target** | Consider 2 GLDs from different classes*, OR Intensification of insulin (if already on insulin) |
| **>2% above target** | Initiation of insulin ± GLP1-RA, OR Intensification of insulin |

*\*Maximum OGLDs allowed is 5. When on insulin continue metformin/SGLT2-i/DPP4-i/GLP1-RA. Stop SU if on full insulin.*
*\*\*For GLDs — interval before addition of another GLD may need to allow for dose titration and escalation of the current therapy.*

**Reassess HbA1c after 3–6 months** of new therapy.

**Lifestyle modification must be maintained at every juncture of T2DM treatment.** It should include strategies to lose weight, correct diet and incorporate physical activities.

---

### Algorithm 3: Suggested Treatment Approach for Specific Patient Profiles

All patients should be on **Lifestyle Modification + Metformin** (unless intolerant or contraindicated; ½ dose at DKD stage 3B, stop at DKD stages 4–5), and their treatment escalated if HbA1c not at individualised target.

**For Overweight/Obese Patients:**
1. GLP1-RA/SGLT2-i (weight loss through lifestyle modification)
2. SGLT2-i (if SGLT2-i given, add GLP1-RA or vice versa)
3. DPP4-i (if not on GLP1-RA) OR SU*
4. Basal OR premixed insulin (escalate to basal bolus/switch to analogues)

**For Normal Weight Patients:**
1. DPP4-i OR SU*
2. SGLT2-i
3. GLP1-RA
4. SU* (if DPP4-i given)
5. TZD
6. Basal OR premixed insulin (escalate to basal bolus/switch to analogues)

**For Patients with Increased Risk of Hypoglycaemia:**
1. DPP4-i
2. SGLT2-i
3. GLP1-RA
4. Low dose SU*
5. Basal OR premixed insulin (escalate to basal bolus/switch to analogues)

**For DKD Stage 3–5α:**
1. SGLT2-i (stop when initiating dialysis)
2. GLP1-RA (contraindicated at eGFR <15 ml/min/1.73 m²)
3. DPP4-i (dose adjustment required)
4. SU* (not advisable in Stages 4–5)
5. Basal OR premixed insulin (escalate to basal bolus/switch to analogues)

**For High Risk CVD (Primary Prevention, without clinical CV events):**
1. GLP1-RA* (choose GLP1-RA with proven CV benefit)
2. SGLT2-i
3. DPP4-i (if not on GLP1-RA) OR SU*
4. TZD
5. Basal OR premixed insulin

**For ASCVD (Established Atherosclerotic CVD):**
1. SGLT2-i / GLP1-RA (if SGLT2-i given, add GLP1-RA or vice versa)
2. GLP1-RA (if not on SGLT2-i) OR SU* (if DPP4-i given)
3. DPP4-i (if not on GLP1-RA) OR SU*
4. TZD
5. Basal OR premixed insulin

**For Heart Failure:**
1. SGLT2-i
2. GLP1-RA
3. DPP4-i (if not on GLP1-RA) OR SU* (if DPP4-i given)
4. Basal OR premixed insulin

*Notes:*
- *\*SU refers to 2nd generation sulphonylurea.*
- *Saxagliptin (SAVOR-TIMI) showed increased risk of hospitalisation for heart failure.*
- *Choose GLP1-RA with proven CV benefit and appropriate label indication for CV reduction.*
- *SGLT2-i recommended in DKD stage 3–5 for renoprotective effects; do not initiate when eGFR <30 ml/min/1.73m².*
- *eGFR levels at which SGLT2-i can be initiated may be subject to change as new evidence becomes available.*
- *α SGLT2-i recommended in DKD stage 3–5.*
- *Bariatric surgery may be considered in patients with BMI ≥32 kg/m² and whose T2DM cannot be controlled by lifestyle changes and pharmacotherapy.*

### Table 3-5: Targets for Control

| Parameters | Target Levels |
|---|---|
| **Fasting or pre-prandial** | 4.4 mmol/L–7.0 mmol/L |
| **Post-prandial** | 4.4 mmol/L–8.5 mmol/L |
| **HbA1c** | <7.0% (for most); ≤6.5% (selected patients) |
| **Triglycerides** | ≤1.7 mmol/L |
| **HDL-C** | Male: >1.0 mmol/L; Female: >1.2 mmol/L |
| **LDL-C** | ≤2.6 mmol/L |
| **BP** | 130–139/70–79 mmHg |
| **Exercise** | 150 minutes/week |
| **Body weight** | Up to 10% weight loss in 6 months if overweight/obese |

### Table 3-6: Individualised HbA1c Targets

| ≤6.5% (Tight) | 6.6%–7.0% | 7.1%–8.0% (Less tight) |
|---|---|---|
| Newly/recently diagnosed; younger age; no CVD; no hypoglycaemia risk | All others | Elderly; advanced CVD/HF/DKD/dementia; hypoglycaemia unawareness; short life expectancy |

### LDL-C Targets in T2DM

| Risk Category | Patient Characteristics | Target LDL-C |
|---|---|---|
| Very high risk | Diabetes + established CVD, or target organ damage, or ≥3 risk factors | <1.4 mmol/L |
| High risk | Diabetes ≥10 years without target organ damage AND any additional risk factor | <1.8 mmol/L |
| Moderate risk | <50-year-old with T2DM of <10 years duration without other risk factors | <2.6 mmol/L |

### Key OGLD Properties for Patient-Specific Selection

| Drug Class | Key Advantage | Key Disadvantage | Use When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metformin | Weight neutral, low hypo risk | GI side effects, avoid in severe CKD | First line for all T2DM |
| SU | Cheap, effective | Hypoglycaemia, weight gain | Cost constraints; add-on |
| DPP4-i | Weight neutral, safe in CKD | Moderate efficacy | Elderly; CKD; HF (avoid saxagliptin) |
| SGLT2-i | Weight loss, CV/renal benefit | UTI/genital infections, cost | ASCVD; HF; DKD; obesity |
| GLP1-RA | Weight loss, CV benefit | Injection, nausea, cost | ASCVD; obesity; high CV risk |
| Insulin | Unlimited efficacy | Hypoglycaemia, weight gain | HbA1c >10%; symptomatic; all stages |

### Grading System

| Grade | Description |
|---|---|
| **Grade A** | Evidence from multiple randomised controlled trials or meta-analyses |
| **Grade B** | Evidence from limited clinical trials or observational studies |
| **Grade C** | Consensus of expert opinion |
