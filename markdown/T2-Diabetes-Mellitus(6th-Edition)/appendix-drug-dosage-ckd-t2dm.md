*GFR in ml/min/1.73 m²; usual dose not maximum dose.*

**Important Notes:**
- Sulphonylureas should be used cautiously because of the increased risk of hypoglycaemia. Glibenclamide is generally avoided due to long half-life and risk of hypoglycaemia in CKD patients. Gliclazide and glipizide are the preferred agents among the second-generation sulphonylureas as they do not have active metabolites and have lower risk of hypoglycaemia in CKD patients.
- Metformin is eliminated via kidney and may accumulate in body as kidney function deteriorates, increasing risk of lactic acidosis.
- SGLT2-i — eGFR below which this class of agents can be prescribed is expected to change, with the advent of their significant reno-protective and cardio-protective benefits; however, the indication may be for their reno and cardio-protective benefits rather than specifically for glucose lowering. At lower eGFR, glucose-lowering efficacy of SGLT2-i are modest.

## Appendix 8: Cardiovascular Outcomes Trials (CVOTs) Summary

### A. DPP4-i CVOTs

| Trial | Drug | n | Population | Primary outcome | Key findings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SAVOR-TIMI** | Saxagliptin | 16,492 | T2DM + history of or multiple risk factors for CVD | 3-point MACE HR 1.00 (0.89–1.12) | Non-inferior; **increased HF hospitalisation** (HR 1.27, p=0.007) |
| **EXAMINE** | Alogliptin | 5,380 | T2DM + ACS within 15–90 days | 3-point MACE HR 0.96 (95% UL ≤1.16) | Non-inferior |
| **TECOS** | Sitagliptin | 14,671 | T2DM + pre-existing CVD | 4-point MACE HR 0.98 (0.89–1.08) | Non-inferior; no increased HF risk |
| **CARMELINA** | Linagliptin | 6,979 | T2DM + established CV and/or prevalent CKD | 3-point MACE HR 1.02 (0.89–1.17) | Non-inferior; albuminuria progression reduced (HR 0.86, p=0.003) |
| **CAROLINA** | Linagliptin vs Glimepiride | 6,033 | Early T2DM + CVD or CV risk factors | 3-point MACE HR 0.98 (0.84–1.14) | Non-inferior; eliminated stigma of CV risk with SU |

**Dedicated HF Trials:**
- **DAPA-HF** (Dapagliflozin): HFrEF patients (42% with T2DM) — CV death or HF hospitalisation HR 0.74 (0.65–0.85)
- **EMPEROR-Reduced** (Empagliflozin): HFrEF patients (50% with T2DM) — CV death or HF hospitalisation HR 0.75 (0.65–0.86)

**Combined meta-analysis (DAPA-HF + EMPEROR-Reduced):** HR 0.75 (0.68–0.84, p<0.0001) vs. placebo for CV death or HF hospitalisation.

## Appendix: Grading System Used Throughout This CPG

### Grade of Recommendation (American Heart Association system)

| Grade | Description |
|---|---|
| **Grade A** | Evidence from multiple randomised controlled trials (RCTs) or meta-analyses demonstrating consistent benefit. The highest level of evidence. |
| **Grade B** | Evidence from limited clinical trials, non-randomised studies or observational studies. A moderate level of evidence. |
| **Grade C** | Consensus of expert opinion, case studies, or standard of care in the absence of strong evidence. The lowest level of evidence. |

### Level of Evidence (Canadian Task Force on Periodic Health Examination)

| Level | Source |
|---|---|
| **Level I** | Evidence from at least one properly designed randomised controlled trial |
| **Level II-1** | Evidence from well-designed controlled trials without randomisation |
| **Level II-2** | Evidence from well-designed cohort or case-control analytic studies |
| **Level II-3** | Evidence from multiple time series or dramatic results in uncontrolled experiments |
| **Level III** | Opinions of respected authorities, based on clinical experience, descriptive studies or reports of expert committees |
