> **Context:** Best practices for supportive care including dental, nutritional, and family planning for NPC patients.

## 7.1 Dental Care

| Assessment Focus | Clinical Management | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Timing** | Must be addressed early, *prior* to radiotherapy, to allow sufficient healing time for any procedures. | Level III |
| **Comprehensive Review** | Includes medical/dental history, extra/intra-oral exam, and necessary radiographs. | |
| **Patient Guidance** | Emphasize oral/denture hygiene care and the use of ultra-soft toothbrushes. | Level III |
| **Referrals** | Mandatory referral for thorough dental assessment and treatment. | |

## 7.2 Treatment of Otitis Media with Effusion

Otitis media with effusion (OME) is a common complication in NPC patients.

**Post-Irradiation OME Treatment Options:** [Level I]
1. Myringotomy plus grommet insertion
2. Simple aspiration (auripuncture)
3. Tympanic membrane fenestration with cauterization

| Procedure | Clinical Efficacy | Complication Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Myringotomy + Grommet** | Higher cure rate at the end of 2-year follow-up (51%) | Higher incidence of complications (28.9%) |
| **Simple Auripuncture + Aspiration**| Lower cure rate (38%) | Lower incidence of complications (15.6%) |

*(Significance: Myringotomy + grommet yields significantly better outcomes despite higher complication risk; p=0.011)*

## 7.3 Contraception

| Patient Status | Recommended Methods | Avoidable Methods |
|---|---|---|
| **Active Cancer or treated in last 6 months** | Highly effective reversible contraceptives (intrauterine or implantable). | Avoid combined hormonal contraceptives (oestrogen + progestin) due to increased Deep Venous Thromboembolism (VTE) risk. |
| **Cancer-free for >6 months** | Any method of contraception is acceptable. | *Assuming no history of hormone-mediated cancer, chest wall irradiation, anaemia, osteoporosis, or VTE.* |

## 7.4 Nutritional Support

NPC patients are at high risk for malnutrition due to the disease process or treatment complications. Adequate nutrition (oral, enteral, or parenteral) facilitates the treatment process and improves quality of life. 

**Recommended Nutritional Supplement Types:**
- Nutritionally complete supplements
- Energy and protein supplements
- Carbohydrate supplements
- Protein supplements
- Fat supplements
