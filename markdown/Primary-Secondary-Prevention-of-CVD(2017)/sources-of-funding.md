## SOURCES OF FUNDING

The development of the CPG was funded through education grants from AstraZeneca, Boehringer Ingelheim, Novartis, Pfizer and Servier provided to National Heart Association of Malaysia. The views and interests of the funding body did not influence the content of the guideline.
