## Key Points for Section 7

1. **A written summary of the pre-anaesthetic assessment, orders or arrangements should be documented in the patient's anaesthetic record.**

2. **Instructions for ward staff must be written clearly and include all necessary preoperative preparations.**

3. **Documentation should be legible and include the anaesthesiologist's name and signature.**

---

## 7.1 Documentation Requirements

A **written summary** of the pre-anaesthetic assessment, orders or arrangements should be documented in the patient's anaesthetic record.

---

## 7.2 Ward Staff Instructions

Instructions for the ward staff with regards to the preparation of the patient for OT must be **written clearly**.

### Essential Documentation Items

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Investigations** | Repeat investigations if necessary |
| **Blood products** | GXM blood or blood products - type of blood product and quantity |
| **Fasting** | Time to commence fasting especially for children |
| **Medications** | Medications to be continued or omitted on the day of surgery |
| **Premedicant drugs** | Dose, route, and time of administration |

---

## 7.3 Legibility and Signature

Documentation should be:
- **Legible**
- Include the anaesthesiologist's **name**
- Include the anaesthesiologist's **signature**

---

## 7.4 Pre-operative Fasting Guidelines

### Paediatric Fasting (College of Anaesthesiologists Guideline 2008)

| Substance Ingested | Fasting Time (hours) |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| **Clear fluids*** | 2 |
| **Breast milk** | 4 |
| **Formula milk, solids** | 6 |

*Clear fluids include water, glucose drink, cordial drink, clear fruit juice*

### Adult Fasting

| Substance | Fasting Time |
|-----------|--------------|
| Clear liquids | 2 hours |
| Light meal/toast | 6 hours |
| Heavy/fatty meal | 8 hours |

> **Important:** Fasting times should be clearly communicated to both patients and ward staff to prevent cancellation of cases.
