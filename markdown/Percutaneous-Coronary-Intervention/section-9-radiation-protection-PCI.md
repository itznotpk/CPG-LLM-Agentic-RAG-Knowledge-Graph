> **Context:** Radiation protection for PCI (2009 Malaysian CPG). Interventional cardiologists are the largest users of medical radiation. Principle: ALARA — reduce exposure to patients, medical staff, and operators to as low as reasonably achievable.

The largest source of radiation comes from medical radiation and the largest users of medical radiation are interventional cardiologists. It is important to be know the biohazards of radiation.Interventional cardiologists should be aware of radiation protection. This entails reducing the radiation exposure to as low a level as reasonably achievable to patients, medical staff and themselves.
