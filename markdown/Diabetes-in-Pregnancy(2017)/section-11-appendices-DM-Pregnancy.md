## Appendix 4C: Initiating Insulin Therapy In Pregnancy

### Insulin Dose By Trimester

| Pregnancy Gestation | Total Daily Insulin Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1st trimester | 0.7 units/kg/day |
| 2nd trimester | 0.8 units/kg/day |
| 3rd trimester | 0.9 units/kg/day |

### Initiation Doses Based On Glycaemic Abnormality

| Glycaemic Abnormality | Suggested Insulin Type and Dose |
|---|---|
| FPG >5.3 mmol/L | Start 0.2 units/kg of intermediate-acting insulin at bedtime; increase by 2 units every 3 days until targets are reached |
| 1-hour postprandial >7.8 mmol/L or 2-hour postprandial >6.7 mmol/L | Start 6 units of short-acting insulin; increase by 2 units every 3 days until targets are reached. If preprandial short-acting insulin dose exceeds 16 units TDS, consider adding 6–10 units intermediate-acting insulin in the morning and titrate accordingly |

### Blood Glucose Targets In Pregnancy

| Timing | Target Value (mmol/L) |
|---|---|
| Fasting or preprandial | ≤ 5.3 |
| 1-hour after the start of a meal | ≤ 7.8 |
| 2-hours after the start of a meal | ≤ 6.7 |

## Appendix 5: Preparation Of Insulin Infusion For Active Labour

### Principles Of Infusion

Glucose is infused intravenously at a fixed rate. Insulin is administered intravenously at a variable rate. CBG is checked hourly and insulin infusion rate adjusted accordingly to maintain the target CBG range of **4.0–7.0 mmol/L**.

Women with T1DM need to have some insulin in their system at all times to avoid diabetic ketoacidosis. Women with T2DM or GDM may or may not require insulin infusion. Insulin requirements decrease after delivery of the placenta.

### Intravenous Insulin Infusion Protocol

Dilute insulin to a concentration of **1 unit/mL**. Use either:
- 20 units short-acting human insulin (actrapid) made up to 20 mL with 0.9% saline in a 20 mL syringe, **OR**
- 50 units short-acting human insulin made up to 50 mL with 0.9% saline in a 50 mL syringe.

Administer insulin infusion via a **syringe pump**.

### Dextrose-KCl Mixture Based On Serum Potassium (K+)

| K+ Result | Amount of KCl to add to 500 mL of 5% Dextrose Solution |
|---|---|
| <3.5 mmol/L | 3 g (39 mmol) |
| 3.5–4.5 mmol/L | 2 g (26 mmol) |
| >4.5 mmol/L | 1 g (13 mmol) |

*Note: One ampoule of 10 mL KCl 10% contains 13.4 mmol of K+.*

- Initiate IV insulin infusion as per the titration table in Section 6 / Algorithm C.
- Maintain a **constant infusion of dextrose-KCl over 6 hours** at a rate of **83 mL/hour**.
- The patient requires one dedicated IV cannula with both infusions running concurrently via infusion pumps, connected via a **3-way stopcock** to the IV cannula.

## Appendix 3D: Sample Menu (For 2000 kcal/Day)

*This sample menu provides approximately 48% carbohydrate, 18% protein and 34% fat from energy.*

*\*This is a menu planned for women with a normal pre-pregnancy BMI. Patients who are underweight, overweight or obese should be referred to a dietitian/nutritionist. The same applies to those with excessive gestational weight gain.*

| Suggested Meal Time | Suggestion for Serving Size and Foods |
|---|---|
| Breakfast (8–9 am) | • 1 cup wheat noodles or spaghetti, preferably soup rather than fried, OR 1 piece chapatti/2 pieces idli with dhal curry, OR 5 tbsp oats with 1 tbsp powder milk and 1 tbsp fruit pieces AND • 1 glass (250 mL) milk |
| Morning Tea (10–11 am) | • 1 piece curry puff, OR egg sandwich (2 pieces whole grain bread, 1 boiled egg, salads), OR 1 container plain yogurt, OR 1 glass milk |
| Lunch (12–2 pm) | • 2 ladles of rice (preferably low-to-medium GI) • ½ piece fish (asam rebus or soup) • ½ cup tempeh/tauhu (preferably baked) • 1 ladle of vegetables • ½ piece guava, 1 slice papaya, 1 apple/orange/pear/lai • Plain water |
| Afternoon Tea (4–5 pm) | • ½ cup barley water and 1 chickpea, OR 1 plain yogurt, OR 4–5 pieces cream cracker with low fat cheese, OR 1 glass milk |
| Dinner (6–8 pm) | • Same as lunch, OR 1 cup spaghetti (bolognaise or soup) with salads and 1 fruit exchange (apple/orange/pear), OR noodles soup with sliced chicken, salads, and 1 fruit exchange, OR chicken sandwich (2–3 pieces whole grain bread, 1 slice baked chicken, salads) with 1 fruit exchange |
| Supper (9–10 pm) | • 1 glass milk |
