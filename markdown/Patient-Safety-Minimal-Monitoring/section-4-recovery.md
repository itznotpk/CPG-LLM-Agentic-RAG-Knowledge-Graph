## Key Points for Section 4

1. **Recovery from anaesthesia must take place in an appropriately staffed and equipped designated area.**
2. **Monitoring standards in recovery must align with the patient's clinical condition.**
3. **There must be a proper handover of care from the operating room staff to recovery staff, and from recovery staff to the ward.**
4. **Discharge from the recovery area requires a review by the anaesthesiologist.**

---

## 4.1 Recovery Area Requirements

a. **Designated Area:** Recovery of the patient from anaesthesia should be carried out in a designated area (i.e., the recovery area / bay) which is appropriately staffed and equipped.

b. **Staffing Ratio:** The minimum staffing ratio should be appropriate for the planned number of beds that are operational.

### 4.2 Minimum Facilities for Recovery Area

| Facility Type | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Beds / Trolleys | Suitable beds or trolleys capable of a head-down tilt |
| Oxygen | Oxygen supply with appropriate delivery equipment (e.g., masks, laryngeal masks, endotracheal tubes) |
| Anaesthesia Capability | Facilities for anaesthetizing patients if necessary (e.g., anaesthetic machine) |
| Ventilation | Facilities for ventilation if necessary |
| Resuscitation | Equipment and drugs for resuscitation, including access to a **defibrillator** |
| Monitoring | Easy access to monitoring equipment similar to the operating room |
| Suction | Suction apparatus |
| Temperature Management | Patient warming devices (e.g., forced air warmer, radiant heater) and temperature monitor devices |
| Fluid Management | Fluid and blood warming devices |
| Staffing | Permanent and dedicated staff |
| Communication | Facilities for easy and rapid communication to summon medical help |

---

## 4.3 Monitoring in Recovery

All patients in the recovery area / bay must be **appropriately monitored** according to the patient's condition.

### Applicable Intraoperative Monitoring Standards (from Section 3)

| Parameter | Monitoring Method |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Oxygenation** | Clinical observation (mucous membranes, operative site) + **Pulse oximetry (mandatory)** with audible variable pulse tone and low alarm limit. |
| **Circulation** | Observation of pulse, heart rate, and blood pressure (measured and recorded regularly). **ECG** should be continuously displayed to provide early warning of impending circulatory failure. |
| **Ventilation** | Clinical observation (chest wall excursion, reservoir bag movement, auscultation, tidal volume). **Capnography** is mandatory if the patient is intubated or under general anaesthesia to quantitatively assess ventilation and correct airway device placement. |
| **Temperature** | Continual monitoring is required for neonates, paediatric patients, and all patients receiving active warming (e.g., forced air warmer, radiant heater). |

---

## 4.4 Handover and Discharge

a. **Proper Handover:** There should be a proper handover for the transfer of the patient's care:
   - From operating room staff to recovery area / bay staff
   - From the recovery area / bay staff to the ward staff

b. **Handover Instructions:** The handover of the patient's care should include clear instructions on:
   - Monitoring for that patient
   - Line of management for unexpected and expected complications

c. **Discharge from Recovery:**
   - **Review:** The patient must be reviewed by the **anaesthesiologist** before discharge from recovery.
   - **Scoring System:** The use of a recovery scoring system is encouraged.

---

## 4.5 Emergency Medical Assistance

**Medical assistance** should be immediately available in case of an emergency in the recovery area.

---

## 4.6 Transport of Patient

a. **From the Operation Theatre to the Recovery Area:**
   - **Supervision:** Transfer of the patient from theatre to recovery should be under the supervision of the anaesthetist.
   - **Oxygen:** Supplemental oxygen should be provided, if necessary.
   - **Equipment:** The bed or trolley should have supportive side rails and a mechanism for placing the patient in a head-down position.

b. **From the Recovery Area to the Ward or Intensive Care Unit:**
   - **Checklist:** A checklist should be established to document that the patient is fit to be discharged from the recovery area safely.
   - **Handover:** The patient should be received by a qualified nurse during the handover and with documentation.
