## Key Points for Section 8

1. **Pre-anaesthetic consultation assesses and ensures the patient is optimised before surgery.**
2. **Consultation should occur at an appropriate time before surgery to allow for adequate assessment and optimisation.**
3. **A comprehensive review of medical history, medications, allergies, physical examination, and investigations is required.**
4. **An informed consent must be taken detailing the anaesthetic technique and associated risks.**

---

## 8.1 Consultation Objectives and Qualifications

While the main purpose of the consultation is to assess and ensure that the patient is **optimised before surgery**, it also includes other aspects of care. The skills and judgment required are different and additional to those involved in the administration of anaesthesia.

a. The pre-anaesthetic consultation should preferably be performed by the **anaesthesiologist who is to administer the anaesthetic**.

b. If that is not possible, there must be some means for the findings of the consultation to be conveyed to the anaesthesiologist administering the anaesthetic.

---

## 8.2 Timing of Consultation

a. The consultation should take place at an **appropriate time before surgery and anaesthesia** to allow for:
   - Adequate assessment
   - Reviewing results of investigations ordered
   - Optimisation of medical conditions

b. This is especially important if there are significant co-morbidities, patients undergoing major surgery, or where there are specific anaesthetic concerns.

c. The pre-anaesthetic consultation may be performed in the **Anaesthetic clinic, ward, or at the operating theatre**.

### Daycare and Same-Day Admission Surgery

For daycare surgery or day of admission surgery, the pre-anaesthetic consultation should **preferably be done prior to admission** (e.g., Anaesthetic Clinic). Otherwise, there should be allowance of adequate time for assessment prior to surgery.

---

## 8.3 Elements of Pre-anaesthetic Consultation

The pre-anaesthetic consultation should include the following:

- **Medical History:** Relevant present and past medical history.
- **Medication Review:** Review of current and past medication, including any herbal medication.
- **Allergies:** Assessment of drug and environmental allergies.
- **Physical Examination:** A clinical examination of the patient.
- **Investigations:** Review of laboratory, radiological, and other investigations. If necessary, the anaesthesiologist may request further investigations.
- **Therapeutic Measures:** If necessary, measures should be ordered and carried out to optimise the patient.
- **Anaesthetic Plan:** Discussion on the anaesthetic plan, technique, and pain management with the patient and/or guardian.

---

## 8.4 Informed Consent and Multidisciplinary Care

a. An **informed consent** should be taken which encompasses details of the anaesthetic technique, risk of anaesthesia, and any other risk relevant to the patient's condition and type of surgery.

b. There should be **consultation with colleagues in other disciplines** where appropriate.

---

## 8.5 Documentation

A written summary of the pre-anaesthetic consultation should be available and becomes part of the patient's medical records.
