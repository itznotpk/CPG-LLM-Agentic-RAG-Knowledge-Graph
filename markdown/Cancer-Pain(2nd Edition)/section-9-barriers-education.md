## 9.1 Barriers

Barriers to the effective management of cancer pain need to be identified and addressed.

### Barrier Categories

| Stakeholder | Key Barriers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Healthcare providers** | Fear of drug addiction, tolerance and AEs of opioids; negative attitudes and lack of knowledge | SR |
| **Patients** | Fear and beliefs about opioids, psychological/physiological well-being, adherence issues, reluctance to report pain | SR; caregiver study |
| **Family caregivers** | Receipt of inadequate information, inappropriate/ineffective communication from healthcare team, concurrent responsibilities, lack of pain-related knowledge and skills | Caregiver study |
| **General public** | Negative attitudes and lack of knowledge towards cancer pain management | SR |

---

### Local Evidence (Hospital Umum Sarawak, Malaysia — Cross-Sectional Study)

Using the BQ-II questionnaire across four domains of patient-related barriers:

| Domain | Ranking (Highest to Lowest Median Score) |
|---|---|
| **Fatalism** | Highest barrier |
| **Harmful effects** | 2nd |
| **Physiological effects** | 3rd |
| **Communication** | Lowest barrier |

**Significant associations:**
*   Education level, gender and marital status had significant impact on various barrier domains
*   Ethnicity had no significant impact on all four domains

---

### Multinational Evidence (Cross-Sectional Survey)

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Strongest attitudinal barrier (across all countries) | Fear of addiction to opioids |
| Weakest attitudinal barrier | Fatalism |
| Higher barrier scores associated with | Older age, male gender, higher pain severity/interference, lower Karnofsky scores, shorter duration of opioid use |

---

### Depression and Barriers

In a multicentre cross-sectional study, depression was significantly associated with total barrier score to cancer pain management.

> **Clinical Note:** Screening and treatment of depression should be an important component of successful pain management.

*   It is important to identify and address barriers to effective cancer pain management.

## 9.2 Education

Education on issues related to cancer pain is an essential element to effective cancer pain management. This involves patients, carers, family and healthcare providers.

### Key Educational Focus Areas

| Focus Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Understanding cancer pain | Nature and impact of cancer pain |
| Disease processes | Relationship between disease and pain |
| Pain assessment | How to describe and document pain appropriately |
| Pain management | Available treatment approaches |
| Analgesic awareness | Knowledge of available analgesics |
| Opioid fears | Dispelling fears regarding opioid analgesia |
| Help and support | Accessing help: when, where and who |
