## Overview
The TNM classification system for breast cancer staging is based on the AJCC Cancer Staging Manual, 8th Edition (2017). This appendix provides the complete definitions for the primary tumour (T), regional lymph nodes (N), and distant metastasis (M) categories.

---

## Definition Of Primary Tumour (T) — Clinical And Pathological
| T Category | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **TX** | Primary tumour cannot be assessed |
| **T0** | No evidence of primary tumour |
| **Tis (DCIS)** | Ductal carcinoma in situ |
| **Tis (Paget)** | Paget disease of the nipple NOT associated with invasive carcinoma and/or DCIS in the underlying breast parenchyma |
| **T1** | Tumour ≤20 mm in greatest dimension |
| — T1mi | Tumour ≤1 mm |
| — T1a | Tumour >1 mm but ≤5 mm (round any 1.0–1.9 mm measurement to 2 mm) |
| — T1b | Tumour >5 mm but ≤10 mm |
| — T1c | Tumour >10 mm but ≤20 mm |
| **T2** | Tumour >20 mm but ≤50 mm |
| **T3** | Tumour >50 mm |
| **T4** | Tumour of any size with direct extension to chest wall and/or skin (ulceration or macroscopic nodules); dermis invasion alone does not qualify as T4 |
| — T4a | Extension to chest wall (pectoralis muscle invasion without chest wall invasion does NOT qualify) |
| — T4b | Ulceration and/or ipsilateral macroscopic satellite nodules and/or oedema (including peau d'orange) not meeting criteria for inflammatory carcinoma |
| — T4c | Both T4a and T4b |
| — T4d | Inflammatory carcinoma |

> **Note:** Lobular carcinoma in situ (LCIS) is a benign entity and is removed from TNM staging in the AJCC 8th Edition.

## Definition Of Regional Lymph Nodes — Clinical (cN)
| cN Category | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **cNX** | Regional lymph nodes cannot be assessed (e.g. previously removed) |
| **cN0** | No regional lymph node metastases (by imaging or clinical examination) |
| **cN1** | Metastases to movable ipsilateral Level I, II axillary lymph node(s) |
| — cN1mi | Micrometastases (approximately 200 cells, >0.2 mm, but none >2.0 mm) |
| **cN2** | Metastases in ipsilateral Level I, II axillary lymph nodes that are clinically fixed or matted; OR in ipsilateral internal mammary nodes without axillary metastases |
| — cN2a | Fixed to one another (matted) or to other structures |
| — cN2b | Only in ipsilateral internal mammary nodes without axillary metastases |
| **cN3** | Metastases in ipsilateral infraclavicular (Level III axillary) lymph node(s) ± Level I, II involvement; OR in ipsilateral internal mammary node(s) with Level I, II metastases; OR in ipsilateral supraclavicular node(s) |
| — cN3a | Ipsilateral infraclavicular lymph node(s) |
| — cN3b | Ipsilateral internal mammary and axillary lymph node(s) |
| — cN3c | Ipsilateral supraclavicular lymph node(s) |

> **Note:** Add (sn) and (f) suffixes to denote confirmation by sentinel node biopsy or FNA/core needle biopsy respectively.

---

## Definition Of Regional Lymph Nodes — Pathological (pN)
| pN Category | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **pNX** | Regional lymph nodes cannot be assessed (not removed or previously removed) |
| **pN0** | No regional lymph node metastasis or ITCs only |
| — pN0(i+) | ITCs only (malignant cell clusters ≤0.2 mm) |
| — pN0(mol+) | Positive molecular findings by RT-PCR; no ITCs detected |
| **pN1** | Micrometastases; or metastases in 1–3 axillary lymph nodes; and/or clinically negative internal mammary nodes with micro/macrometastases by SLN biopsy |
| — pN1mi | Micrometastases (approximately 200 cells, >0.2 mm but none >2.0 mm) |
| — pN1a | Metastases in 1–3 axillary nodes, at least one >2.0 mm |
| — pN1b | Metastases in ipsilateral internal mammary sentinel nodes, excluding ITCs |
| — pN1c | pN1a and pN1b combined |
| **pN2** | Metastases in 4–9 axillary lymph nodes; or positive ipsilateral internal mammary nodes by imaging without axillary metastases |
| — pN2a | Metastases in 4–9 axillary nodes (at least one deposit >2.0 mm) |
| — pN2b | Metastases in clinically detected internal mammary nodes ± microscopic confirmation; with pathologically negative axillary nodes |
| **pN3** | Metastases in ≥10 axillary nodes; or in infraclavicular (Level III) nodes; or positive ipsilateral internal mammary nodes by imaging + ≥1 positive Level I/II axillary nodes; or >3 axillary nodes + micro/macrometastases by SLN biopsy in clinically negative ipsilateral internal mammary nodes |
| — pN3a | ≥10 axillary nodes (at least one >2.0 mm); or metastases to infraclavicular (Level III) nodes |
| — pN3b | pN1a or pN2a + cN2b (positive internal mammary by imaging); or pN2a + pN1b |
| — pN3c | Metastases in ipsilateral supraclavicular lymph nodes |

> **Note:** Add (sn) and (f) suffixes to denote confirmation by sentinel node biopsy or FNA/core needle biopsy respectively, with NO further resection of nodes.

---

## Definition Of Distant Metastasis (M)
| M Category | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **M0** | No clinical or radiographic evidence of distant metastases |
| **cM0(i+)** | No clinical/radiographic evidence of distant metastases in the presence of tumour cells/deposits ≤0.2 mm detected microscopically or by molecular techniques in circulating blood, bone marrow or other non-regional nodal tissue in an asymptomatic patient |
| **M1** | Distant metastases detected by clinical and radiographic means (cM) and/or histologically proven metastases >0.2 mm (pM) |
