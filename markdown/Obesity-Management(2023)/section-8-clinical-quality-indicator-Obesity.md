To assist in the implementation of the clinical practice guidelines (CPG), the following are proposed as clinical audit indicators for quality management:

**1. Weight Reduction Indicator**
Percentage of people with obesity (at 6 months follow-up) who reduced weight by ≥5% = (Number of people with obesity with weight loss ≥5% / Total number of people with obesity at 6 months follow-up attending the facility) × 100%

Proposed target: 50% for primary care and tertiary care

**2. Comorbidity Screening Indicator**
Percentage of people with obesity screened for complications = (Number of people with obesity screened for complications / Total number of people with obesity attending the facility) × 100%

Proposed target: 85% for primary care and tertiary care

The targets are proposed based on existing evidence, taking into account the practicality of the recommendations and the reality of the current available resources and facilities.
