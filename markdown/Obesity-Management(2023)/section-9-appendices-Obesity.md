## Appendix 2: Binge Eating Scale (BES)

The Binge Eating Scale is a 16-item questionnaire completed by the patient to assess the presence of binge eating behaviours which may be indicative of an eating disorder. Each question presents groups of statements; the patient selects the statement that best describes how they feel. Clinicians should use this tool to screen for binge eating disorder in patients with obesity.

Key domains assessed:
- Self-consciousness about weight
- Eating speed and stuffed feeling
- Control over eating urges
- Eating when bored
- Eating when not physically hungry
- Guilt or self-hate after overeating
- Loss of control when dieting
- Frequency of large quantity eating
- Compensatory calorie restriction
- Control of compulsion to eat
- Stopping when full
- Hiding eating behaviour
- Calorie cycling/meal skipping patterns
- Preoccupation with food
- Food cravings intensity
- Recognition of hunger signals

## Appendix 4: Blood Pressure Tables For Children

Blood pressure in children should be interpreted using **height-age-sex percentile normalised BP tables** (refer to full Appendix 4 in the original CPG).

**Key thresholds for diagnosis of hypertension in children:**
- Diagnosis must be based on **3 separate measurements at least 1 week apart**
- Prehypertension: ≥90th percentile for age, sex, and height
- Stage 1 hypertension: ≥95th percentile
- Stage 2 hypertension: ≥95th percentile + 12 mmHg

**Note:** The 90th percentile is 1.28 SD, 95th percentile is 1.645 SD, and the 99th percentile is 2.326 SD over the mean.

*Full tables for boys and girls (ages 1–17) stratified by BP percentile (50th, 90th, 95th, 99th) and height percentile (5th to 95th) are available in Appendix 4 of the original CPG document.*
