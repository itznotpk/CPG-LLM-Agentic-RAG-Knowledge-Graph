## 19. Eisenmenger Syndrome in PAH in Congenital Heart Diseases

Patients with Eisenmenger syndrome may remain stable for many years. The main principle in the management of Eisenmenger patients is not to destabilize the balanced physiology and risk reduction strategies.

### 19.1 General principle

#### 19.1.1 General Advice

- **a.** Patients with Eisenmenger syndrome should *AVOID* the following:
  - Pregnancy
  - Dehydration
  - Moderate to severe strenuous exercise, particularly isometric exercise
  - Acute exposure to excessive heat (e.g. Hot tub, sauna)
  - Chronic high-altitude exposure
  - Smoking
- **b.** Air travel in commercial airlines is not discouraged in stable Eisenmenger patients but care should be taken to avoid dehydration and inactivity during the flight.
- **c.** All medications given to patients with Eisenmenger syndrome should be scrutinized carefully to prevent potential alteration in the balanced haemodynamic and adverse effects on renal and hepatic systems.
- **d.** Long term oxygen therapy for at least 12 to 15h/day may improve symptoms but has not been shown to influence survival.
