## Purpose

This appendix provides the **LAST Management Protocol** for emergency treatment of local anaesthetic systemic toxicity.

---

## Recognition of LAST

### Early Warning Signs (CNS)
- Circumoral numbness/tingling
- Metallic taste
- Tinnitus
- Visual disturbances (blurred vision, diplopia)
- Dizziness/light-headedness
- Agitation/confusion
- Drowsiness

### Progressive Signs
- Muscle twitching
- Tremors
- Slurred speech
- **Seizures**
- Loss of consciousness

### Cardiovascular Signs
- Hypotension
- Bradycardia
- Arrhythmias (including VT/VF)
- Cardiac arrest

> **Warning:** Cardiovascular collapse can occur without warning CNS symptoms, especially with bupivacaine.

---

## IMMEDIATE MANAGEMENT

### Step 1: STOP Local Anaesthetic Administration

### Step 2: Call for HELP
- Alert the team
- Request lipid emulsion 20%
- Prepare for resuscitation

### Step 3: Maintain Airway
- Administer 100% oxygen
- Secure airway if needed
- Hyperventilate to correct acidosis

### Step 4: Treat Seizures (if present)
| First Line | Alternative |
|------------|-------------|
| Midazolam | Propofol (small doses) |
| | Thiopental |

**Avoid large doses of propofol** in cardiovascular compromise

### Step 5: Cardiovascular Support

**For hypotension/bradycardia:**
- Standard resuscitation measures
- Avoid vasopressin, calcium channel blockers, beta-blockers, or local anaesthetic

**For cardiac arrest:**
- Start CPR
- Use standard ACLS protocols with modifications
- **Reduce adrenaline doses** (≤1 mcg/kg)
- **Avoid amiodarone and lidocaine**

---

## LIPID EMULSION 20% THERAPY

### Indications
- Cardiovascular instability
- Cardiac arrest
- Severe/prolonged symptoms

### Dosing Protocol

| Phase | Dose |
|-------|------|
| **Initial bolus** | 1.5 ml/kg over 1 minute |
| **Infusion** | 0.25 ml/kg/min |

### Repeat Bolus
If cardiovascular instability persists after 5 minutes:
- Give **two further boluses** at 5-minute intervals
- **Maximum total bolus:** 3 ml/kg

### Increase Infusion (if needed)
- Double the rate to **0.5 ml/kg/min**

### Maximum Dose
- **Total lipid emulsion dose:** 12 ml/kg

### Duration
- Continue infusion for at least **10 minutes** after cardiovascular stability restored

---

## DOSING REFERENCE TABLE (70 kg Adult)

| Component | Dose |
|-----------|------|
| **Initial bolus** | 105 ml over 1 min |
| **Infusion rate** | 18 ml/min (initial) |
| **Max bolus total** | 210 ml |
| **Max total dose** | 840 ml |

---

## MODIFIED CPR CONSIDERATIONS

| Standard | LAST Modification |
|----------|-------------------|
| Adrenaline 1 mg | **≤1 mcg/kg (≤100 mcg for 70 kg patient)** |
| Amiodarone | **AVOID** |
| Lidocaine | **AVOID** |
| Calcium channel blockers | **AVOID** |
| Beta-blockers | **AVOID** |
| Vasopressin | **AVOID** |

**Prolonged resuscitation may be required** - do not abandon prematurely

---

## POST-RESUSCITATION CARE

1. **ICU admission** for monitoring (minimum 12 hours)

2. **Blood sampling** for LA levels if available

3. **Document incident** thoroughly

4. **Report to risk management**

5. **Consider underlying cause** - catheter migration, inadvertent IV injection

---

## PREPARATION CHECKLIST

Ensure the following are immediately available in all locations where LA is used:

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Lipid emulsion 20%** | Minimum 500 ml |
| **Airway equipment** | Full resuscitation kit |
| **Suction** | Available and working |
| **Oxygen** | High flow capability |
| **Benzodiazepines** | For seizure control |
| **ACLS drugs** | Standard resuscitation drugs |
| **Defibrillator** | Readily accessible |
