## Purpose

This appendix contains the **Malignant Hyperthermia Reporting Form** for voluntary reporting of suspected MH cases to the Department of Anaesthesiology & Critical Care, Hospital Kuala Lumpur (HKL).

---

## When to Use This Form

### Reporting Criteria (from Section 5.2.3)

**All suspected MH with Larach's score-rank 4 and above should be reported** to the MH database in HKL.

## Form Contents

The MH Reporting Form typically requires:

### Section A: Patient Information
- Name, IC number, date of birth
- Age, sex, weight
- Contact details
- Known family history of MH or anaesthetic complications

### Section B: Clinical Details
- Date of incident
- Type of surgery/procedure
- Hospital and location

### Section C: Trigger Exposure
- Volatile agents used (and duration of exposure)
- Suxamethonium use
- Time from exposure to first signs

### Section D: Clinical Features
- ETCO₂ values (baseline and peak)
- Heart rate (baseline and peak)
- Temperature (baseline and peak)
- Muscle rigidity
- Arrhythmias
- Metabolic acidosis/respiratory acidosis
- Myoglobinuria
- Elevated CK

### Section E: Larach Score
Larach's Clinical Grading Scale for MH:

| Rank | Likelihood |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Almost never MH |
| 2 | Unlikely MH |
| 3 | Somewhat less than MH |
| 4 | Somewhat greater than MH |
| 5 | Very likely MH |
| 6 | Almost certain MH |

**Report if Rank 4 or above**

### Section F: Treatment Given
- Dantrolene administered (dose, timing, source)
- Cooling measures
- Other supportive treatment
- Outcome

### Section G: Follow-Up
- Patient counselled (yes/no)
- Alert card/letter issued
- Genetic testing/IVCT planned

---

## Additional Actions Required

1. **Issue alert card or letter** to patient and family regarding suspected MH

2. **Counsel patient and family** on:
   - Confirmation of MH diagnosis by genetic testing
   - In vitro contracture testing (not available in Malaysia)
   - Family screening

3. **Update patient's medical records** with MH alert
