## Purpose

This appendix contains the **Mast Cell Tryptase (MCT) Test Request Form** used when sending blood samples to the Institute for Medical Research (IMR) following a suspected perioperative anaphylaxis.

---

## When to Use This Form

Use this form when:
1. **Perioperative anaphylaxis is suspected**
2. Blood samples have been collected according to the MCT sampling protocol
3. Samples need to be sent to IMR for analysis

---

## MCT Sampling Protocol (from Section 5.1.3)

| Sample | Timing |
|--------|--------|
| **First sample** | Following stabilisation, within 60 min up to 3 hours post reaction |
| **Second sample** | 24 hours post reaction |

### Sample Handling

- Place blood in **plain serum tubes**
- Label with **date and time** of sampling
- Send immediately to hospital pathology lab for proper storage
- Store at **4°C for not longer than 4 days** before sending to IMR

---

## Form Contents

The MCT Test Request Form typically requires:

| Field | Information Required |
|-------|---------------------|
| **Patient identification** | Name, IC/passport number, date of birth |
| **Hospital details** | Hospital name, department, contact person |
| **Clinical information** | Type of reaction, timing, treatment given |
| **Sample details** | Date and time of collection, sample number |
| **Requesting physician** | Name, designation, contact details |

---

## Submission Details

**Institute for Medical Research (IMR)**
- Coordinate with hospital pathology laboratory for sample transport
- Ensure samples are appropriately stored during transport
- Allow adequate time for test processing and results reporting
