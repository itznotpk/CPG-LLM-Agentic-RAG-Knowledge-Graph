## Purpose

This appendix contains the **Hospital Kuala Lumpur Anaesthetic Allergy Clinic (HKLAAC) Referral Form** used for referring patients who have experienced suspected perioperative hypersensitivity reactions for formal investigation.

---

## When to Use This Form

### Indications for Referral (from Section 5.1.4)

- Unexplained cardiac arrest during anaesthesia
- Unexplained, unexpected hypotension requiring active treatment (especially if only responded to adrenaline)
- Unexplained, unexpected bronchospasm with significant desaturation, relatively resistant to treatment
- Generalised rash, flushing or urticaria
- Exhibited angioedema

## Referral Process

### Step 1: Initial Consultation
Make telephone call to the anaesthesiologist in charge of the clinic to ascertain indication of the referral.

### Step 2: Email Documentation
Send email to hklaac@gmail.com with:
- Completed HKLAAC referral form
- Supporting documentation (listed below)

---

## Required Documentation

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Completed referral form** | All fields filled in legibly |
| **Description of reaction** | Detailed timeline and symptoms |
| **Blood test details** | MCT sampling dates and times |
| **Contact information** | Anaesthesiologist, surgeon, patient (at least 2 phone numbers) |
| **GA form** | Legible softcopy |
| **Vital signs chart** | During and after reaction |
| **Recovery room/PACU chart** | Post-operative documentation |
| **Drug charts** | All medications administered |

## Form Contents

The HKLAAC Referral Form typically requires:

### Section A: Patient Information
- Name, IC number, date of birth
- Contact details (multiple phone numbers)
- Known allergies

### Section B: Referring Hospital/Doctor
- Hospital name and department
- Referring anaesthesiologist details
- Surgeon details

### Section C: Incident Details
- Date and time of reaction
- Type of surgery/procedure
- Anaesthetic technique used
- Medications administered with timing
- Description of reaction
- Treatment given

### Section D: Laboratory Results
- MCT sample timing and results (if available)
- Other relevant investigations

### Section E: Current Status
- Patient's current condition
- Any ongoing treatments

---

## After Referral

1. **Skin testing performed 4-6 weeks post reaction** (risk of false negatives if earlier)

2. **Report issued** to patient and referring anaesthesiologist/surgeon after testing

3. **Patient receives:**
   - Medic Alert bracelet application form
   - ADR and Allergy Card application forms for Pharmacy Department
