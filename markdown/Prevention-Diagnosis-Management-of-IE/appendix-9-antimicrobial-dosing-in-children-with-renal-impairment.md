## Appendix 9: Antimicrobial dosing in children with renal impairment

## Estimating creatinine clearance in paediatrics

There are 2 methods to calculate CrCl in children. Available data suggest that the bias of Schwartz calculated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) increases at lower levels of GFR. The Schwartz and Counahan-Barratt formulae can provide rapid and convenient estimates of GFR, although clinicians should be aware of their imprecision in this setting. 1
