## Summary
67F with known non-valvular AF (CHA₂DS₂-VASc 4) on warfarin (INR 2.4) who has undergone primary PCI with DES for NSTEMI, also with T2DM. Requires a tailored antithrombotic strategy balancing thromboembolic risk, stent thrombosis risk, and bleeding risk with triple therapy. A proton pump inhibitor should be co-prescribed for gastric protection.
