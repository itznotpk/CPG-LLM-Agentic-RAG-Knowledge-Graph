## Summary
62-year-old male with newly diagnosed HFrEF (LVEF 25%, NYHA II), Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, and obesity (BMI 32 kg/m²). Two CPGs apply and conflict on first-line therapy: T2DM CPG prefers sulfonylurea (gliclazide) as first-line OGLD, but HFrEF CPG and T2DM CPG both contraindicate sulfonylureas in heart failure due to increased morbidity and hypoglycaemia risk. The contraindication wins; gliclazide must be stopped and replaced with an SGLT2 inhibitor (dapagliflozin or empagliflozin) which has proven HFrEF benefit. Metformin may be continued given eGFR 58 mL/min/1.73m².
