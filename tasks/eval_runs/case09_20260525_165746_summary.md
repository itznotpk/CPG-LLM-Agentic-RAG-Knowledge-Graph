## Summary
67-year-old female with known non-valvular AF (CHA₂DS₂-VASc = 4) on warfarin (INR 2.4) who has undergone PCI with DES for NSTEMI, also with Type 2 Diabetes. Triple antithrombotic therapy is required — balancing stroke prevention (VKA), ACS management (aspirin), and DES patency (clopidogrel) against the significant major bleeding risk of combining warfarin with dual antiplatelet therapy. The CPGs note this is an area with limited evidence and require individualised risk-benefit assessment.
