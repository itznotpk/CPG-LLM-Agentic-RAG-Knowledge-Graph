## Summary
35-year-old primigravida at 30 weeks gestation with chronic hypertension (currently on Losartan 50mg OD, which is contraindicated in pregnancy) and newly diagnosed GDM (FPG 7.4 mmol/L, 2h OGTT 11.2 mmol/L). BP 158/104 mmHg confirmed on two readings 4 hours apart — meets criteria for hypertensive crisis if persistent >15 minutes. No proteinuria, no signs of pre-eclampsia currently. Two CPGs apply and conflict on first-line therapy: Hypertension in Pregnancy CPG mandates stopping ARB and switching to methyldopa/labetalol/nifedipine; Diabetes in Pregnancy CPG addresses GDM management. The ARB contraindication wins; losartan must be stopped immediately and replaced with a pregnancy-safe antihypertensive.

## Safety Flags
- [CRITICAL/llm] Losartan - CRITICAL contraindication in pregnancy
  Losartan (an ARB) is a known teratogen and is critically contraindicated in the second and third trimesters of pregnancy (30 weeks gestation) due to severe fetal risks including renal/tubular dysplasia, oligohydramnios, and growth retardation.
- [MAJOR/graph] Angiotensin Receptor Blocker - contraindicated with caution
  Angiotensin Receptor Blocker contraindicated with Pregnancy. Evidence: Angiotensin Converting Enzyme Inhibitors (ACE-I) and Angiotensin Receptor Blockers (ARB) are contraindicated in pregnancy.
- [MAJOR/graph] Arb - contraindicated with caution
  Arb contraindicated with Pregnancy. Evidence: ACE-I, ARB, ARNI, MRA, SGLT2-i and ivabradine are contraindicated in pregnancy.
