## Summary
67-year-old female with known non-valvular atrial fibrillation (CHA2DS2-VASc 4) on warfarin (INR 2.4) who has undergone PCI with a drug-eluting stent for NSTEMI, with concurrent Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus. This patient requires a carefully balanced antithrombotic strategy managing the dual risk of stent thrombosis and thromboembolism against a significant bleeding risk from triple therapy (VKA + aspirin + clopidogrel).
