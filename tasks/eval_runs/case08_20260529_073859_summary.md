## Safety Flags
- [MAJOR/llm] ACE inhibitor + MRA - hyperkalemia and AKI risk
  Concomitant initiation of an ACE inhibitor and an MRA in a patient with eGFR 58 mL/min significantly increases the risk of hyperkalemia and acute kidney injury, requiring close monitoring.
- [MODERATE/llm] Dapagliflozin/Empagliflozin + Gliclazide - hypoglycemia risk
  Adding an SGLT2 inhibitor to gliclazide increases the risk of hypoglycemia; consider reducing the gliclazide dose or discontinuing it, especially given the patient's HFrEF.
- [MODERATE/llm] Gliclazide - potential for adverse effects in HFrEF
  Sulfonylureas like gliclazide may be associated with adverse cardiovascular outcomes in patients with heart failure, and continuing it alongside new HFrEF therapies (SGLT2i, beta-blocker) increases hypoglycemia risk.
- [MODERATE/llm] Beta-blocker + Gliclazide - masked hypoglycemia symptoms
  Beta-blockers can mask the adrenergic symptoms of hypoglycemia, making it harder for the patient to recognize low blood sugar, especially when also on gliclazide and an SGLT2 inhibitor.
