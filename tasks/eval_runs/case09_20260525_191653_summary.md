## Summary
67-year-old female with known non-valvular atrial fibrillation (CHA₂DS₂-VASc = 4) on warfarin (INR 2.4) status post successful PCI with drug-eluting stent for NSTEMI, with concurrent Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus. This is a complex antithrombotic scenario requiring careful balancing of AF stroke prevention (OAC), acute coronary syndrome treatment (antiplatelet therapy), and post-DES stent thrombosis prevention. Triple therapy (VKA + aspirin + clopidogrel) is the initial recommended strategy with transition to dual therapy, with INR intensity reduction to 2.0–2.5 during triple therapy period.
