## Safety Flags
- [MAJOR/llm] Enalapril + Spironolactone - hyperkalemia and AKI risk
  Concomitant use of an ACE-inhibitor (enalapril) and a mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (spironolactone) in a patient with eGFR 58 mL/min significantly increases the risk of hyperkalemia and acute kidney injury.
- [MODERATE/llm] Enalapril + Gliclazide - hypoglycemia risk
  ACE-inhibitors can enhance the hypoglycemic effect of sulfonylureas, increasing the risk of low blood sugar when starting enalapril with gliclazide.
- [MODERATE/llm] Dapagliflozin + Gliclazide + Metformin - hypoglycemia risk
  Adding an SGLT2 inhibitor (dapagliflozin) to existing metformin and gliclazide therapy increases the risk of hypoglycemia, requiring close glucose monitoring.
- [MODERATE/llm] Gliclazide - contraindication in HFrEF
  Sulfonylureas like gliclazide are generally discouraged in patients with heart failure due to potential for adverse cardiovascular outcomes and increased mortality, especially in the context of HFrEF.
