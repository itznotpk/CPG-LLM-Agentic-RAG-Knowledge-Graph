## Summary
62-year-old male with newly diagnosed HFrEF (LVEF 25%) and comorbid Type 2 Diabetes (HbA1c 8.4%, eGFR 58 mL/min/1.73m²) and obesity (BMI 34). Currently on metformin 1g BD and gliclazide MR 60mg OD. Patient is clinically stable and euvolemic. Evidence strongly supports initiation of foundational HFrEF therapies (ACE-I/ARNI, beta-blocker, SGLT2-i) and modification of the anti-diabetic regimen with an SGLT2 inhibitor for dual cardiometabolic benefit. Metformin can be continued given eGFR >30 mL/min/1.73m² but requires monitoring given eGFR 58 mL/min/1.73m².

## Monitoring
- Renal profile (creatinine, eGFR, potassium): Within 2-4 weeks of ACE-I/ARB initiation or dose escalation, then at least annually target=eGFR stable; potassium <5.6 mmol/L; reduce/discontinue ACE-I if creatinine rises ≥30% from baseline, eGFR reduces ≥25%, or potassium ≥5.6 mmol/L
- HbA1c: Every 3 months until target, then every 6 months target=Individualised target range of 7%-8% while minimising adverse effects particularly hypoglycaemia
- Urine albumin-creatinine ratio (UACR): At baseline, then at least twice yearly if abnormal; annually if normal target=Detection and quantification of albuminuria for DKD risk stratification
- Serum creatinine and eGFR: At least annually; recheck 2-4 weeks after ACE-I initiation target=eGFR >30 mL/min/1.73m² for continued metformin; ≥30 mL/min/1.73m² for SGLT2-i initiation
- Blood pressure: At every visit; ambulatory or home BP monitoring encouraged target=130-139/70-79 mmHg with coronary artery disease; SBP not <120 mmHg and DBP not <70 mmHg in DKD
- LDL-cholesterol: At baseline and after statin initiation, then annually target=According to CV risk category; proven clinically meaningful CV risk reduction
- Body weight and BMI: At every visit target=Weight reduction to improve CV risk factors, glycaemic control and reduce HF risk
