## Summary
67-year-old female with non-valvular atrial fibrillation (CHA₂DS₂-VASc 4) on warfarin (INR 2.4), presenting with NSTEMI status-post primary PCI with drug-eluting stent, and concomitant Type 2 Diabetes. This complex dual-pathology case requires a tailored antithrombotic strategy balancing thrombotic risk (AF stroke risk + stent thrombosis) against bleeding risk on triple therapy. She is at high stroke risk and high bleeding risk given age >65 and triple antithrombotic combination.
