Description: Anorgasmia is characterised by the absence or marked infrequency of the orgasm experience or markedly diminished intensity of orgasmic sensations. In women, this includes a marked delay in orgasm, which in men would be diagnosed as Male Delayed Ejaculation. The pattern of absence, delay, or diminished frequency or intensity of orgasm occurs despite adequate sexual stimulation, including the desire for sexual activity and orgasm, has occurred episodically or persistently over a period at least several months, and is associated with clinically significant distress.
Inclusions: Psychogenic anorgasmy
