## Red flags
- Sudden loss of vision in one or both eyes: stop PDE5i immediately and seek urgent medical care — possible NAION
- Erection lasting >4 hours: seek emergency treatment immediately — risk of priapism
- Sudden decrease or loss of hearing: stop PDE5i and seek prompt medical attention
- New or worsening angina symptoms: urgent cardiac review before considering nitrate discontinuation
- Severe hypotension: if PDE5i accidentally co-administered with nitrates, seek emergency care immediately
