## Summary
35-year-old primigravida at 30 weeks with pre-existing chronic hypertension (on losartan 50mg OD) and newly diagnosed gestational diabetes mellitus (fasting glucose 7.4 mmol/L, OGTT 2h 11.2 mmol/L). BP 158/104 mmHg confirmed — hypertensive urgency range. Two CPGs apply and conflict on first-line therapy: The Hypertension CPG (Table 15) lists methyldopa as first-line antihypertensive in pregnancy, but the current losartan (ARB) is absolutely contraindicated in pregnancy. The Diabetes in Pregnancy CPG identifies metformin as first-line OAD for GDM after failed diet/exercise, but also flags FPG ≥7.0 mmol/L as an indication to start insulin. Urgent losartan cessation and antihypertensive switch required; specialist co-management for both conditions.
