## Safety Flags
- [MAJOR/llm] Ramipril + Spironolactone - hyperkalemia and AKI risk
  Concurrent initiation of an ACE inhibitor (ramipril) and a mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (spironolactone) significantly increases the risk of hyperkalemia and acute kidney injury, especially in patients with heart failure.
- [MODERATE/llm] SGLT2 inhibitor + Gliclazide - hypoglycemia risk
  Starting an SGLT2 inhibitor while the patient is still on gliclazide (a sulfonylurea) increases the risk of hypoglycemia; close monitoring of blood glucose is required until gliclazide is discontinued.
- [MAJOR/llm] Gliclazide - contraindication in HFrEF
  Gliclazide, a sulfonylurea, is associated with increased cardiovascular risk and may worsen outcomes in patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (LVEF 25%).
